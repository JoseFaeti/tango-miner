[Grammar shadow prototype](../grammar-shadow-prototype.md)

## 〜ても (concession)

Split て/で + も following a continuative verb, adjective or auxiliary and a following clause can yield “even if; even though”. The attached predicate remains available as vocabulary. Supported 〜てもいい permission forms use their own card. Copular でも, merged and sentence-final analyses are not covered; other meanings and contextual ambiguity remain, and precision and recall have not been independently evaluated. Dictionary expression precedence can suppress a match and reservation can affect lexical frequencies.

## 〜なら

The card matches split hypothetical copular なら after an adjacent finite
predicate, noun, pronoun, na-adjective or supported predicate + の, when a
following clause is present. The antecedent remains lexical; longer dictionary
expressions can take precedence. Sentence-final uses, merged tokenizations and
other analyses remain unclassified. Topic and conditional readings are context
dependent; precision and recall have not been independently evaluated.

## 〜ば

The card matches a split conjunctive ば following an adjacent hypothetical
verb, adjective or auxiliary when a following clause is present. The predicate
remains lexical. Supported complete obligation forms are handled by their own
card, and longer dictionary expressions can take precedence. Sentence-final
uses, merged tokenizations and other analyses remain unclassified; contextual
precision and recall have not been independently evaluated.

## 〜ことにする

The card identifies a decision to do or not do something after a finite verb
or supported verbal negative, followed by merged ことにする or split こと +
に + する. Selected present, past and polite positive endings are supported.
The predicate remains available as a lexical candidate; the matched suffix is
reserved, subject to dictionary expression precedence. Noun-based uses,
continuing ことにしている, negative endings and other analyses remain lexical.
A decision reading is context dependent; precision and recall are not measured.
