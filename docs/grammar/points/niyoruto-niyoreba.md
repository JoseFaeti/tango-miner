# 〜によると / 〜によれば

Status: [~]

## Meaning

According to.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + によると / によれば, using the
Sudachi verb lemma よる and particle endings. The source noun remains available
as vocabulary. Tests cover both forms, pronouns, reservation and card identity,
as well as 寄る, whitespace and neighboring によって / による forms.

The surface pattern cannot distinguish an attribution from a conditional
meaning of よる (depend on); ambiguous matches remain review candidates. Kanji
spellings and other tokenizations are not covered.
