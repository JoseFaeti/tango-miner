# 〜をよそに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Disregarding; contrary to someone's concerns, expectations, or circumstances.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をよそに, including the spelling を余所に.
The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi full dictionary tests
cover both spellings, boundaries, reservation and card identity. The same token
sequence can mean putting something elsewhere (家をよそに建てた); the matcher
cannot distinguish this literal reading. Alternative tokenizations and other
forms are not covered; further source evidence is needed.
