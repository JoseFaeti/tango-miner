# 〜くする / 〜にする

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Making or choosing.

## Verified coverage

Adjective continuative く and noun or na-adjective に followed by する
match plain, past, polite, negative, connective and conditional forms supported
by the shared verb helpers. For example, 部屋を明るくする makes a room
bright, while お茶にする chooses tea. The adjective is included in the
example span because Sudachi combines it with く; tokens are not reserved,
preserving lexical candidates. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
inflections, competing constructions and incomplete or separated forms.

## Remaining limitations

Noun + に + する can be semantically ambiguous, and tokenization and the
shared inflection helpers limit coverage. Merged expressions and other
conjugations are not exhaustively covered. Verbal ことにする and ようにする
have separate cards.
