# 〜以上は

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Given that; now that a situation holds, a consequence or commitment follows.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches 以上 with optional adjacent は following an adjacent attributive verb,
  adjective or auxiliary, including past, negative, な and である forms. The
  card reserves only 以上 and any は, retaining the preceding predicate.
- Focused Sudachi split-mode C tests cover these forms, numeric and pronominal
  uses, spacing, card evidence and reservation. Dictionary expression precedence
  may suppress a match. The matcher does not verify the following consequence;
  comparative uses after predicates can also match. Other tokenizations,
  kana spelling and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unmeasured.
