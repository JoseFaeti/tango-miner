# 〜に応えて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In response to; to meet a request or expectation.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に応えて, including kana にこたえて and
polite に応えまして. The stable card identity is 〜に応えて. Only the phrase
beginning at に is reserved; the preceding noun remains lexical evidence.
Sudachi full-dictionary tests verify these forms, boundaries, lexical controls,
reservation, example surface and registry/legacy identity integration.

## Limitations

Kana spelling is ambiguous: physical effects such as 暑さが身にこたえて also
match. No semantic noun whitelist is imposed. Written 答えて (answer a question)
and 応じて belong to other lexical or grammar readings and do not match.
Finite に応える and attributive に応えた are outside this rule's scope.
The [kana specification](nikotaete-c1a6fb62.md) shares this rule and card identity.
Tests also verify polite にこたえまして and a single card retaining both kana
and kanji example surfaces. Other tokenizations remain unverified.
