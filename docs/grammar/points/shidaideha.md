## Meaning

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by separately tokenized 次第 or しだい
  and the particles で and は produces a card. The marker and particles are
  reserved; the preceding word remains available. Focused tests cover Sudachi
  mode C, both spellings, card evidence, and lexical and succession controls.
- Combined tokens such as 結果次第 are not split. Other tokenizations and
  semantic ambiguities remain unverified.
