# 〜限りでは

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As far as the available evidence or scope indicates.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent predicate or noun plus の followed by 限りでは or かぎりでは
  produces a card. The preceding evidence remains available as lexical material.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, spacing, reservation and identity.
- An immediately following ない or ある is excluded to avoid copular uses
  such as 嬉しい限りではない. This also misses evidence-scope uses with those
  words, and the matcher cannot reliably distinguish literal limits from scope.
  Other variants and contextual distinctions remain unverified.
