# 〜かのように

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As if.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent かのように following a finite or attributive verb,
  adjective or auxiliary; the preceding predicate remains lexical evidence.
- Focused Sudachi full tests cover reservation, card examples, ordinary ように,
  鹿のように, and the separate かのようだ and かのような constructions.
- Other tokenizations and spelling variants remain unverified; the rule does
  not determine whether the comparison is hypothetical.
