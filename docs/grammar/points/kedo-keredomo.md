# 〜けど / 〜けれども

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Contrast or softening.

## Verified behavior

- A terminal verb, adjective, or auxiliary followed immediately by the
  conjunctive particle けど or けれど produces a card, including sentence-final
  and polite, past, negative, and copular forms. Adjacent particle も extends
  けれど to けれども. Only the marker is reserved; the predicate remains
  available as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests exercise Sudachi forms, a sentence-initial conjunction, a
  lexical control, token adjacency, reservation, and card examples. The marker
  may express contrast, softening, or background context; the rule does not
  disambiguate meaning. Analyzer segmentation and colloquial variants outside
  these forms remain limitations.
