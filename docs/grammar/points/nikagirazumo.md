# 〜に限らず〜も

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not only but also.

## Verified coverage

Matches noun or pronoun + に限らず followed immediately by noun or pronoun +
も, including kana spelling of に限らず. The recorded span is に限らず;
both nouns remain lexical evidence. The independent 〜に限らず and 〜も
rules can also match. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries,
reservation and card identity.

Commas, intervening modifiers and other attachments are not yet supported.
The rule does not determine whether the following も is semantically related.
