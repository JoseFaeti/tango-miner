# 〜限りの

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As much as possible, or to the limit of an amount or capacity, modifying a noun.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent affirmative predicate or noun plus の followed by 限りの or
  かぎりの produces a card. The preceding words remain lexical evidence;
  focused tests cover Sudachi forms, spacing, reservation and identity.
- Sudachi keeps できる限り as a single lexical token, so できる限りの is
  not matched. Other tokenizations and contextual distinctions remain
  unverified; noun phrases can have literal limit readings.
