# 〜んだけど / 〜んですが

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Explanatory background or a softened introduction, sometimes with explicit
contrast. Context determines whether the connective is contrastive or softening.

## Verified coverage

Matches explanatory んだ / んです and のだ / のです followed immediately
by けど, けれど, けれども or が, including nominal and adjectival
predicates with な. The source span covers the explanatory particle through
the connective, leaving the predicate available as lexical evidence. The
existing 〜んだ and 〜けど rules can also record overlapping occurrences;
the pipeline does not choose between those grammar cards by registry order.

Focused tests use Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization for casual, polite,
nominal and uncontracted forms, plus controls for 読んだ, plain けど and
possessive のです. Reservation, identity and lexical retention are tested.

## Limitations

Coverage depends on the explanatory rule and Sudachi token boundaries;
unusual analyses and other connectives are not established. The rule does not
infer discourse intent. No corpus-level precision or recall assessment was run.
