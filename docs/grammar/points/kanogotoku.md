## Meaning

As if; literary.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent かのごとく and かの如く after a finite or attributive
  verb, adjective, or auxiliary; the preceding predicate remains lexical evidence.
- Sudachi full tests cover both spellings, reservation and card examples, and
  distinguish ごとき, ordinary ごとく, and かのように.
- Other tokenizations and variants remain unverified; hypothetical meaning is
  not determined from context.
