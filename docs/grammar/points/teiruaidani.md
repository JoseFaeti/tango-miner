# 〜ている間に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

During an ongoing action or state, another event occurs.

## Verified behavior

- A verbal ている / でいる or contracted てる / でる followed by 間に or あいだに
  produces this card. The progressive and 間に are reserved; the attached
  verb remains available as evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C,
  adjacent boundaries, controls, card identity and reservation.
- Past, negative and other inflected progressive forms are not yet supported.
  The matcher does not distinguish temporal 間 from all spatial or other
  readings in context. Corpus precision and recall have not been measured.
