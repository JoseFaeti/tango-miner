# 〜ないことには

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Unless; a condition must be satisfied before the following outcome is possible.

## Coverage

Recognizes adjacent `ないことには` with Sudachi's negative auxiliary or
adjective ない, including verb, adjective, copular and existence predicates.
The matched span starts at ない, leaving the preceding predicate available as
lexical evidence. The ordinary verbal 〜ない rule may also report the negative.

Focused analyzer tests cover these predicate types, adjacent token boundaries,
nearby constructions, reservation and registry identity. The matcher does not
inspect the following clause: nominal uses such as a topic about something not
being done can be indistinguishable and may be reported as grammar. Other
spellings and analyzer segmentations have not been verified.
