# 〜を最後に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

After which no more.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by separately tokenized を最後に or
  をさいごに produces a grammar card, including before して. The expression is
  reserved while the anchor remains available as lexical evidence. Focused
  tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, spelling, boundaries, lexical controls,
  reservation and card identity.
- Nominal adjacency does not establish that the anchor is a time or event or
  that the activity ends: for example, 彼を最後に紹介する can mean introducing
  him last. Such ambiguous uses can match. Other tokenizations and semantic
  distinctions remain unverified.
