# 〜あげく

Status: [~]

## Meaning

After a long process, resulting in an outcome, often an undesirable one.

## Verified behavior

- A past verb or noun plus の followed by separately tokenized あげく or 挙句
  produces a card, including an adjacent に when present. Only the expression
  is reserved; the preceding action or noun remains lexical evidence. Focused
  tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, lexical controls and card evidence.
- Standalone あげく and fused forms such as 挙げ句の果て are not matched.
  Other tokenizations and whether the context describes a prolonged process or
  an undesirable outcome remain unverified; the rule does not infer semantics.
