# 〜ないことはない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not impossible; a qualified affirmation, sometimes expressing reluctance.

## Coverage

Recognizes adjacent `ないことはない`, `ないことはなかった`,
`ないことはありません` and `ないことはありませんでした` after verb,
adjective, copular and existence predicates. The matched span starts at the
first ない, preserving the preceding predicate as lexical evidence. The ordinary
verbal negative rule can also report an overlapping match.

Focused Sudachi tests cover these forms, nearby constructions, token boundaries,
reservation and registry identity. A literal topic about something absent can
be indistinguishable from the grammar reading. Other variants, including
`ないこともない` and contractions, remain unverified and are not matched.
