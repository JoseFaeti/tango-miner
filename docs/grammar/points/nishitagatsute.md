# 〜にしたがって

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As or according to.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by
にしたがって or に従って. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity.
Literal following or obeying, such as 彼にしたがって歩く, also matches; the
meaning cannot be distinguished from grammar by this pattern. The shorter
にしたがい / に従い and other tokenizations are not supported.
