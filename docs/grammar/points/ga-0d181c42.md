# 〜が

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Contrast or introduction.

## Verified behavior

Matches the Sudachi conjunctive particle が immediately after a terminal verb,
adjective, or auxiliary, including polite, past, negative, and sentence-final
forms. The card identity is `〜が (接続助詞)`, distinct from the existing
subject and focus particle card `〜が`. Only が is reserved; predicates remain
lexical evidence. It can overlap the explanatory `〜んだけど / 〜んですが`
card. The rule does not distinguish contrast, introduction, or softening.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, subject and lexical controls, reservation,
identity, and overlap. Sudachi can label a connective as a case particle, for
example in `高いが買う`, so those analyses are missed; accepting all case
particles after predicates would also capture nominalized subject constructions.
Other tokenizations and corpus precision and recall remain unmeasured.
