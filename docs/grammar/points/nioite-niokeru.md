# 〜において / 〜における

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Formal expression meaning in or at a place, time, situation, or field.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + において and における, including the
Sudachi split おけ + る. The noun remains lexical evidence; following は or
の is outside the match. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries,
kanji 置く controls, reservation and identity.

Kana において can also mean literally placing something, so an adjacent
literal reading such as 机において may be matched. Other spellings and
tokenizations remain unverified.
