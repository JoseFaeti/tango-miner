# 〜として

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As or in the capacity of.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun + として, including 〜としての and 〜としては,
while retaining the noun as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi
tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity. A preceding
volitional verb is excluded, leaving 〜ようとする to its own rule.

Noun + として can also have other meanings, including quotation or setting a
condition; this surface matcher cannot distinguish them. Other tokenizations
and non-noun attachments remain unverified. Related longer points such as
〜としても and 〜としては are tracked separately.
