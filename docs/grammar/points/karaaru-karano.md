# 〜からある / 〜からの

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As many as.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches からある and からの after a numeric quantity, including a separate
  counter or a number and counter in one token. The quantity remains outside
  the grammar span. Both verbal and prenominal Sudachi analyses of ある work.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, token reservation and lexical
  controls. Ordinary starting points such as 三時からの会議 are structurally
  ambiguous and also match; the shorter から card can overlap. Other quantity
  spellings, inflected ある, and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unverified.
