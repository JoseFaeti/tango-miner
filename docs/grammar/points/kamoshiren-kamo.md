## Verified behavior

- Split-mode C か + も + しれ / 知れ + ん and sentence-final かも

  expression precedence can suppress a match.
- Focused tests cover analyzer forms, formal and lexical controls, reservation,
  and examples. Attachment is morphological and can be ambiguous. Short かも
  is limited to a sentence boundary or final particle; other contexts and
  tokenizations remain unverified. かもしれぬ is not covered.
