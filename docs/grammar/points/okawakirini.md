# 〜を皮切りに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Starting with the first event or item in a series.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を皮切りに, including the shared
prefix of を皮切りにして, を皮切りにする and を皮切りにした. The
example and reserved span cover を through に; the preceding noun remains
available as a lexical candidate. Focused tests cover Sudachi full-dictionary
tokenization, controls, reservation and identity.

The matcher does not distinguish contextual readings. を皮切りとして and
other spellings or tokenizations, including hiragana かわきり, are not covered;
the point remains partial.
