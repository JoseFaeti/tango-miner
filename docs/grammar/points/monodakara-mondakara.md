# 〜ものだから / 〜もんだから

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An explanatory reason or excuse: because; since. `もんだから` is colloquial.

## Tracked scope

Matches adjacent terminal or attributive verbs, adjectives, and auxiliaries
followed by nominal `もの` or `もん`, copular `だ`, and connective `から`.
The card span covers `ものだから` or `もんだから`. The rule does not reserve
tokens, though overlapping rules can reserve them. Sudachi full-dictionary
tests cover both spellings, past, adjective, na-adjective and noun predicates,
and lexical controls.

Literal references such as `買ったものだから` have the same morphology and
remain review candidates. Merged tokenizations and corpus precision and recall
have not been evaluated.
