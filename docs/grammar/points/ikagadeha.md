## Meaning

## Verified behavior

- Recognizes noun or pronoun plus optional の and いかんでは／如何では,
  including Sudachi's combined 如何で token. The attached noun remains available
  for mining; the related いかんで rule does not claim the shorter span.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, unrelated readings,
  reservation and card identity. Other segmentations and contextual ambiguity
  remain unverified; the spelling 如何 does not establish its reading.
