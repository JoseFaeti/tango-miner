# 〜ずとも

- [Meaning](#meaning)

- [Limitations](#limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even without doing; a literary concessive expression.

## Verified coverage

Matches an irrealis verb followed by separately tokenized auxiliary ず and
particle とも. The grammar span is ずとも; the verb remains vocabulary. Tests
cover Sudachi examples, gaps, lexical controls, reservation and card examples.

## Limitations

Sudachi can label とも as a noun or 来 before ず as a continuative form;
those analyses are not matched. Other tokenizations and auxiliary chains have
not been assessed. A structural match can also occur in longer expressions.
