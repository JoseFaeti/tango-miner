## Meaning

As of; with or by means of, in formal language.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をもちまして / を持ちまして, retaining the
preceding noun as lexical evidence. Sudachi full-dictionary examples and focused
tests cover both spellings, boundaries, reservation, identity and overlap with
〜をもって. Literal possession or carrying can also match; context is not used
to distinguish it. Other spellings and tokenizations remain unverified.
