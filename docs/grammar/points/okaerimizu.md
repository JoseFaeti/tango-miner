# 〜を顧みず

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without regard for; disregarding.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を顧みず, including kana をかえりみず
and optional に. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi
full dictionary tests cover forms, boundaries, neighboring verbs, reservation
and card identity. The matcher cannot distinguish disregard from literal
looking back on the past; kana also cannot distinguish 顧みる from 省みる.
The spelling 省みず, other inflections and alternative tokenizations are not
covered. Further source evidence is needed to assess these boundaries.
