# 〜予定だ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Plan or scheduled event.

## Verified behavior

- An attributive verb, verb-negative ない, or noun + の followed by 予定 and
  finite だ/です, including past だった/でした, produces a card. The 予定
  and copula tokens are reserved; the attached words remain lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, card evidence and unrelated uses.
  Bare or particle-marked 予定, negative copulas and other inflections remain
  outside verified scope.
