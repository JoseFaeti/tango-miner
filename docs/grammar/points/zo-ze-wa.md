# ぞ / ぜ / わ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Sentence-final emphasis: ぞ and ぜ can add assertive or exhortative force;
わ can express emphasis or feeling. Their tone depends on context and dialect.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent Sudachi final-particle tokens ぞ, ぜ and わ after a
  preceding token, including clause-medial discourse uses. Only the particle
  is reserved, preserving the preceding lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover all three forms, lexical and part-of-speech controls,
  whitespace, reservation, card examples and identity registration.
- Initial particles, merged tokenizations and pragmatic distinctions are not
  handled. Dictionary expression precedence can suppress a match; precision
  and recall have not been independently evaluated.
