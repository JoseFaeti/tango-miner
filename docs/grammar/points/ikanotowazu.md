# 〜いかんを問わず

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Regardless of the outcome or circumstances of the preceding noun.

## Verified behavior

- After a noun or pronoun, optionally followed by の, matches いかん／如何を
  問わず, including kana とわず. The noun and の remain lexical evidence;
  the overlapping 〜を問わず card also collects the expression.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, unrelated expressions,
  reservation and identity. The literal negative を問わずに is excluded.
  Other tokenizations and contextual ambiguities remain unverified, and the
  surface pattern cannot establish the reading of 如何. を問わない is not covered.
