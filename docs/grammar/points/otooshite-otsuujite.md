# 〜を通して / 〜を通じて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Through or throughout.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を通して / を通じて, including
means, intermediary and duration examples. The noun remains lexical evidence;
following の is outside the span. Focused tests cover analyzer tokenization,
boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and identity. Literal uses such as
passing an object through something can share this form and may be matched;
the rule does not distinguish senses. Other spellings and tokenizations remain
unverified. Separate entries for the two forms remain scheduled.
