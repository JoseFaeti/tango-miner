# 〜とき

Status: [~]

## Meaning

When.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent attributive predicates and nouns followed by の with とき or 時
  produce a card. Only とき or 時 is reserved; the preceding words and any
  following particle remain available as evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi
  split-mode C, tense, negative and nominal forms, lexical controls and cards.
- Nominal uses may be ambiguous, and bare 時 and other tokenizations remain
  outside the verified scope. The rule does not distinguish temporal senses of
  an attached 時 from other readings in context.
