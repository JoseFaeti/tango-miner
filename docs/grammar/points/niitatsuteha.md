# 〜に至っては

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As for, particularly: singles out a case, often as an extreme example.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by に至っては, including kana
spelling when analyzed as 至る. The preceding expression remains lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation,
identity, and the separate に至って rule. Literal uses can also match; the
rule does not distinguish them from the figurative meaning. Kana forms split
by Sudachi into other lemmas, other attachments and tokenizations remain
unverified.
