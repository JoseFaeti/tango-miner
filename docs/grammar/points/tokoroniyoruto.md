# 〜ところによると

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

According to what one has heard, read, investigated, or otherwise learned.

## Verified behavior

Adjacent attributive predicates followed by ところ/所によると produce a card;
the predicate remains available as vocabulary. Focused tests cover Sudachi
forms, boundaries, reservation, identity and the overlapping 〜によると card.
Literal places and conditional dependence can share this surface pattern and
remain ambiguous review candidates. Other tokenizations and variants, including
ところによれば, remain unverified.
