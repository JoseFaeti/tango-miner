# 〜んじゃない / 〜のではない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Explanatory negation, conjecture or prohibition depending on context.

## Coverage

The rule reuses the explanatory んだ rule's aligned token analyses for
negative んじゃ, んでは, のじゃ and のでは forms. Focused Sudachi tests
cover んじゃない, んじゃなかった, んではありません and のではない,
including conjectural and prohibitive contexts. The recognized span excludes
following か. The same occurrence may also produce the explanatory 〜んだ
card; lexical candidates are retained. The stable identity is
`〜んじゃない / 〜のではない`.

This is a morphological match, not a classification of the intended meaning.
Nominalized readings can be ambiguous. Merged nominalizers, colloquial variants
and other tokenizations outside the shared rule remain unverified; corpus
precision and recall have not been assessed.
