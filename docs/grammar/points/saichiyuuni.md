# 〜最中に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In the middle of.

## Verified behavior

- A noun plus の, or a verb in ている / てる form, followed by 最中に or
  さいちゅうに produces an “in the middle of” card. Only 最中に is reserved;
  the attached expression remains available as evidence. Focused tests cover
  Sudachi split-mode C, nominal and progressive forms, controls, and card evidence.
- The same spelling can mean the confection もなか. Contextual reading is not
  available to this matcher, so an attached nominal confection use can be
  ambiguous. Other spellings, complex predicates and forms without に remain
  unverified.
