# 〜にあたらない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Does not constitute.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent に当たらない and にあたらない after a noun, pronoun or
verb, including tested past, polite negative, continuative and conditional forms.
The preceding expression remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover source
spans, controls, reservation and identity. The separate には form has its own rule.

Literal uses of 当たる, such as not hitting a wall, are also matched; the rule
does not distinguish senses. Other tokenizations and inflections remain unverified.
