# 〜ない限り

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Unless; as long as a condition is not met.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent ない限り and ないかぎり are recognized with Sudachi's negative
  auxiliary or adjective analysis, including verb, adjective, copular and
  existential negatives. The preceding predicate remains lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover tokenization, spacing, reservation, identity and the
  related 〜限り and 〜限りでは rules. Lexical 限りない remains a word.
- Continuations では, だ, です, の and に are excluded to avoid related
  constructions. Other readings and negative variants remain unverified.
