# 〜たりなんかして

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Tentative suggestion or example, often with a casual or playful tone.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent continuative verb, adjective or auxiliary followed by たり or
  だり and なんかして produces a card spanning the particle through して;
  the attached predicate remains available as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, both particle variants, an adjective,
  incomplete and separated forms, and card integration. This is a morphological
  match: context can give the phrase other readings, and other tokenizations or
  variants are not yet covered.
