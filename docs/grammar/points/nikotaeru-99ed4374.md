# 〜に応える

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Meet expectations or requests; respond to a demand.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に応える in dictionary and plain past forms,
ば/たら conditionals, selected negative conditionals, and plain/polite
affirmative, negative and volitional forms. It also covers potential/passive
and causative auxiliaries through dictionary, past, conditional, and selected
plain/polite positive and negative forms, including kana にこたえる. The stable
identity is 〜に応える. The complete predicate beginning at に is reserved;
the preceding noun remains available as lexical evidence. Sudachi full-dictionary
regressions cover these forms, token boundaries, lexical controls, reservation,
example surfaces and registry/legacy identity integration. The connective
に応えて remains with the separate [〜に応えて](nikotaete.md) rule.

## Limitations

Potential/passive readings remain contextually ambiguous, and the kana spelling
can match physical effects such as 暑さが身にこたえる. Causative-passive chains
and other tokenizations or inflections remain unsupported. No semantic noun
whitelist is imposed. Written 答える (answer a question) and 応じる do not
match. Corpus coverage remains unverified.
