# 〜に応える

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Meet expectations or requests; respond to a demand.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に応える in dictionary and plain past forms,
polite affirmative present/past forms, and plain/polite negative present/past
forms, including kana にこたえる. The stable identity is 〜に応える. Only the
phrase beginning at に is reserved; the preceding noun remains available as
lexical evidence. Sudachi full-dictionary regressions cover these forms, token
boundaries, lexical controls, reservation, example surfaces and registry/legacy
identity integration. The connective に応えて remains with the separate
[〜に応えて](nikotaete.md) rule.

## Limitations

Conditional, potential/passive, volitional, causative and other inflections are
not yet covered. Kana spelling is ambiguous: physical effects such as
暑さが身にこたえる also match. No semantic noun whitelist is imposed. Written
答える (answer a question) and 応じる do not match. Other tokenizations and
corpus coverage remain unverified.
