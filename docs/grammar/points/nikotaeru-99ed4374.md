# 〜に応える

- [Meaning](#meaning)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Meet expectations or requests; respond to a demand.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に応える and に応えた, including kana
にこたえる and にこたえた. Both finite and attributive uses share the stable
identity 〜に応える. Only the phrase beginning at に is reserved; the preceding
noun remains available as lexical evidence.
Sudachi full-dictionary regressions cover forms, token boundaries, lexical
controls, reservation, example surfaces and registry/legacy identity integration.
The connective に応えて remains with the separate [〜に応えて](nikotaete.md) rule.

## Limitations

Polite, negative, potential and other inflections are not yet covered.
Kana spelling is ambiguous: physical effects such as 暑さが身にこたえる also
match. No semantic noun whitelist is imposed. Written 答える (answer a question)
and 応じる do not match. Other tokenizations and corpus coverage remain unverified.
