# 〜と

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Conditional or habitual consequence.

## Verified coverage

The existing `〜と` card also matches Sudachi conjunctive `と` after an
adjacent finite verb, adjective, or auxiliary when subsequent sentence content
is present. This includes negative and copular predicates and discovery as well
as habitual consequences. Only the particle is reserved; overlapping longer
rules remain available. Focused analyzer and integration tests cover these
forms and lexical controls.

The card shares its identity with quotation, accompaniment and listing; see
[と](to.md). Token classification cannot establish the precise meaning, and
elliptical sentence-final uses, spacing and particles inside larger tokens are
not covered. Corpus precision and recall have not been measured.
