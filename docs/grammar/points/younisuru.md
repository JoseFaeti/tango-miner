# 〜ようにする

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Make an effort or habit.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent plain nonpast verb or verb + ない followed by ようにする,
ようにした, ようにします or ようにしました. Sudachi full-dictionary
regressions also cover ようにしない, ようにしなかった, ようにしません,
ようにしませんでした and the connective ようにして, including before いる.
The span starts at よう, leaving the preceding verb available as a lexical
candidate. This point owns its overlap with 〜ように; 〜ている can independently
match an overlapping connective. Tests cover noun and adjective attachments,
other following verbs, incomplete forms and separated tokens as controls.

## Remaining limitations

Other inflections and tokenizations are not covered. Morphology alone cannot
distinguish an effort or habit from other verb-attached readings; precision and
recall have not been independently evaluated.
