# 〜につれて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As one change progresses, another change accompanies it.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by kana
につれて. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover
Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity. Kana literal
uses such as 学校につれて行く can also match; meaning is not disambiguated.
Kanji 連れて is excluded to preserve literal uses, so grammar written with kanji
is missed. The shorter につれ and other tokenizations remain unsupported.
