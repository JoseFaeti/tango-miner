- [Meaning](#meaning)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Undeterred by.

## Supported behavior

Matches noun-attached をものともせず and を物ともせず, optionally followed
by に, as one card. The obstacle remains available as a lexical candidate.
Sudachi full dictionary tests cover both spellings, adjacency, unrelated forms,
and reservation and identity integration. The matcher checks token structure,
not whether the preceding noun is semantically an obstacle.

Other inflections such as をものともしない and alternative tokenizations are
not covered. The verified [〜をものともせずに](omonotomosezuni.md) variant shares
this card. Broader inflection and alternative-tokenization coverage remain
unimplemented.
