# 〜限りにおいて

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Insofar as; to the extent that a condition or evidence applies.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent predicate or noun plus の followed by 限りにおいて or
  かぎりにおいて produces a card. The preceding words remain available as
  lexical material. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, spacing, reservation,
  identity and overlap with において.
- The nested において rule can also produce its own card. Literal limits and
  figurative scope are not distinguished; other forms and tokenizations remain
  unverified.
