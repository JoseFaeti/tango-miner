# 〜を境に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Taking a point as a boundary, often marking a change before and after it.

## Implemented coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by を境に, including the shared prefix of
を境にして. The example and reserved span cover を through に; the preceding
noun remains a lexical candidate. Focused tests cover Sudachi full-dictionary
tokenization, controls, reservation and identity. Literal spatial boundaries
also match; the matcher cannot distinguish them from temporal turning points.

Other spellings and tokenizations, and the related を境として form, have not
been implemented or verified; the point remains partial.
