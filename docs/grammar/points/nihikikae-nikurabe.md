# 〜にひきかえ / 〜に比べ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In comparison.

## Verified coverage

The existing [〜にひきかえ](nihikikae.md) and [〜に比べて](nikurabete.md)
cards cover the respective forms without creating a duplicate combined identity.
The latter also matches continuative に比べ and にくらべ, such as
去年に比べ、今年は暑い. The comparison target remains lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover analyzer forms, inflection controls, reservation and identity.
Literal comparison and exchange readings can be ambiguous; other attachments,
inflections and tokenizations remain unverified.
