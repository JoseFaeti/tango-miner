# 〜を前提に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

On the assumption of.

## Implemented coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by を前提に, including the shared prefix
of を前提にして, を前提にする and を前提にした. The card example and
reserved span cover を through に, leaving the preceding noun available for
lexical mining. Focused tests exercise Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization,
boundary controls, reservation and card identity.

The related form を前提として and other spellings or tokenizations are not
covered. Longer continuations are represented by the shared prefix; the point
remains partial.
