# 〜にして初めて

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Only after a milestone; not until.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にして初めて and にしてはじめて after the temporal
expressions supported by [〜にして](nishite.md), leaving the milestone as
lexical evidence. Both grammar cards may be emitted for the same occurrence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, reservation and identity.

Other milestone expressions and tokenizations remain unverified. Literal
にする followed by 初めて can be indistinguishable from this construction.
