# 〜に関して / 〜に関する

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Concerning.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に関して, に関しまして, and に関する.
The preceding noun remains lexical evidence; following は and の are outside
the matched span. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, lexical controls,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Other tokenizations, attachments and
inflected forms remain unverified; に関し is a separate scheduled point.
