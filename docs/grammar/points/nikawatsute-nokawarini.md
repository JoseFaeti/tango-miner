# 〜に代わって / 〜の代わりに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In place of; on behalf of.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に代わって and の代わりに, including
kana spellings. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests
cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, reservation and card identity. The pattern
also matches literal replacement or change, which cannot reliably be separated
by form alone. Other attachments and variants remain unverified; clause +
かわりに is tracked separately.
