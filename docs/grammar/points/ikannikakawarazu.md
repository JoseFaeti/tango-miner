# 〜いかんにかかわらず

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Regardless of the outcome or circumstances of the preceding noun.

## Verified behavior

- After a noun or pronoun, optionally followed by の, the rule recognizes
  いかん／如何にかかわらず, including 関わらず and 拘わらず. The
  attached noun remains available for lexical processing. When の is present,
  the overlapping 〜のいかんにかかわらず card reserves it. The shorter
  〜にかかわらず card also collects the overlapping expression.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, unrelated expressions,
  reservation and identity. Other segmentations and contextual ambiguities
  remain unverified; the surface pattern cannot establish the reading of 如何.
