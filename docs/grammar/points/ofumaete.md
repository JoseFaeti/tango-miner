# 〜を踏まえて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Taking into account.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を踏まえて or をふまえて, retaining the
preceding noun as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, reservation and identity. The rule does not distinguish a literal
use of 踏まえる from taking something into account. Attributive を踏まえた
and other inflections or tokenizations are not covered.
