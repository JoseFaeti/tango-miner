# 〜と考えると

Status: [~]

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Coverage and limitations](#coverage-and-limitations)

## Meaning

Considering a proposition; when one thinks that something is the case.

## Coverage and limitations

The rule recognizes a finite verb, adjective or auxiliary followed by adjacent
`と考えると`, reserving only that expression. Focused Sudachi tests cover plain
and past propositions, a copula, spacing, and unrelated uses of 考える.
The final と can be analyzed as either a connective or a case particle, so
matching both retains analyzer variants but can also admit quotation readings.
Noun-final propositions, polite 考えますと, and other variants are not yet
covered; noun-final と can also mean “with,” so requires further investigation.
