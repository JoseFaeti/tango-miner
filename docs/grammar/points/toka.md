# 〜とか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Quotation or uncertain report.

## Verified coverage

Recognizes adjacent split `と` + `か` particles after a verb, adjective, or
auxiliary, including reported clauses such as `来るとか`, `帰ったとか`,
`高いとか`, and `休みだとか`. Only the marker is reserved; the predicate
remains available as lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi full-dictionary tests
cover these forms, nominal listing, spacing, `なんとか`, and the contracted
auxiliary in `置いとかなきゃ`. Dictionary-expression precedence applies.

The same clause-attached form can also list examples, so this rule does not
resolve that ambiguity. Nominal `とか` is handled by [や / とか](ya-toka.md),
including ambiguous nominal reports. Other tokenizations, punctuation or
spaces between the predicate and marker, and nominal quotation distinctions
are not covered. Corpus precision and recall have not been measured.
