## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に基づいて, including kana spelling. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Attributive に基づいた is covered by the
separate [〜に基づく](nimotozuku.md) rule; other inflections and tokenizations
are not covered by this rule.
