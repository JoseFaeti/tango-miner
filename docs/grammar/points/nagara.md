# 〜ながら

Status: [~]

## Meaning and tracked scope

simultaneous actions; distinguish concessive uses.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent verb continuative and Sudachi's 接続助詞 ながら produce a
  card spanning the particle, with the attached verb retained as lexical
  evidence. A following clause must begin; ながらの and sentence-final
  fragments are excluded. Dictionary expression precedence can suppress a
  match when a complete expression includes the attached word.
- Tests exercise Sudachi split-mode C analyses, lexical controls, incomplete
  examples, source examples and identity protection. The same morphology can
  express concession (知りながら); the card lists both meanings and does not
  distinguish them. Adjective and noun concessive uses, other tokenizations,
  and more reliable semantic disambiguation remain unimplemented.
- The longer 〜ながらも has its own card and takes precedence when matched.
