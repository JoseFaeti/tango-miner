# 〜まま

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Remaining in a state.

## Implemented

The rule reserves the separate まま or 儘 token after an adjacent verb,
adjective, auxiliary, or attributive の / な. This includes past and negative
predicates, as well as noun and adjectival-noun modifiers. The card describes
remaining in the same state; the modifying words remain available as evidence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi full-dictionary examples, lexical controls, token
reservation and card identity.

## Limitations

Sudachi treats そのまま as one lexical token, which is left intact. Other
merged or unusual spellings and contextual ambiguities have not been assessed.
The separate 〜ないまま rule can also match negative examples; 〜ぬまま
remains scheduled for its own specific treatment. Predicate part of speech alone cannot prove the intended
meaning in every context.
