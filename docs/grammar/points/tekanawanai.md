# 〜てかなわない

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An unpleasant situation or feeling is intolerable; cannot bear it.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent て / で and かなわない or 敵わない, including plain past,
なくて, conditional and polite negative forms. The preceding verb, adjective
or adjectival noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi full
tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Kana かなう and 敵う also have lexical senses such as matching an opponent;
morphology alone cannot distinguish those readings. 叶う and 適う spellings
are excluded as lexical controls. Other spellings, literary negatives and
unusual tokenizations remain unverified.
