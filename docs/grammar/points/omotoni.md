## Implemented coverage

Matches noun or pronoun + をもとに, を元に, or を基に, including the
shared prefix of をもとにして, をもとにする and をもとにした. The
card example and reserved span cover を through に; the preceding noun remains
available as a lexical candidate. Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization and
reservation, identity and lexical retention are covered by focused tests.

The sequence is ambiguous: 本を元に戻す (restore a book to its original
state) also matches. The rule cannot distinguish that literal use from a basis
without context. Other spellings, tokenizations and longer forms have not been
verified; the point remains partial.
