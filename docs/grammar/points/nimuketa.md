# 〜に向けた

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Aimed at or intended for a goal or recipient, modifying a following noun.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に向けた / にむけた when followed by a
noun, pronoun, adjective or prenominal modifier. The target and following words
remain lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, reservation,
identity and past-tense controls. Literal physical aiming in an attributive
clause can also match; the rule does not distinguish senses. Other following
forms and tokenizations remain unverified.
