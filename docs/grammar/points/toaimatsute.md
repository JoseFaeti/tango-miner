- [Meaning](#meaning)

## Meaning

Combined with; coupled with, producing a joint effect.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by と相まって, とあいまって,
or と相俟って in the observed split Sudachi tokenization. The preceding noun
remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover spellings, boundaries, reservation
and identity. Other tokenizations, attachments and polite forms remain
unverified; morphology alone cannot establish the intended meaning.
