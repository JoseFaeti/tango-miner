# 〜を中心に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Centered on; mainly involving.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を中心に, including を中心にした;
the noun and following words remain outside the grammar span. Focused tests
cover Sudachi tokenization, lexical controls, reservation and identity.
Literal spatial centers can share this form and are not distinguished.
Other spellings and tokenizations are unverified; を中心とした is not matched.
