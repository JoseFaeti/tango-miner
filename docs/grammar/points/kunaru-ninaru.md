# 〜くなる / 〜になる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Becoming.

## Verified coverage

Adjective continuative く and noun or na-adjective に followed by なる match
plain, past, polite, negative, connective and conditional forms supported by
the shared verb helpers. Examples include 寒くなった and 先生になる. The
adjective is included in the example span because Sudachi combines it with く;
the rule does not reserve its tokens, preserving lexical candidates. Tests use
Sudachi full tokenization and check competing verbal constructions and 鳴る.

## Remaining limitations

Coverage depends on tokenization and does not resolve all semantic ambiguity
of noun + に + なる. Other conjugations and merged expressions are not
exhaustively covered. Verbal なくなる and ようになる have separate cards.
