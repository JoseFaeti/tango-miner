# 〜に足りない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not worth.

## Verified coverage

Matches に足りない after a noun or verb, including past, continuative,
conditional and polite negative forms, when Sudachi identifies 足りる and
its suffix separately. The matched particle and predicate are reserved for
the grammar card; the preceding word remains available. Focused tests cover
examples, reservation, identity, and unrelated or positive 足る forms.

Literal insufficiency, as in 生活に足りない, has the same form and is also
matched; the rule cannot determine whether the context means lack of worth.
Alternative tokenizations and spellings remain unverified.
