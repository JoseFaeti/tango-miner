# 〜ところだ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

About to, in progress or just finished.

## Verified behavior

- A verb in dictionary form, ている form, or past た/だ form followed by
  ところ or 所 and a finite だ/です copula produces a card. Past copulas
  だった and でした are also supported. The rule reserves the noun and
  copula while preserving the attached verb and its lexical evidence. Focused
  tests cover Sudachi tokenization, these forms, controls and card evidence.
- The same surface can describe a place or occasion, such as 食べるところだ;
  the rule does not distinguish these readings. Phrases ending in ところに or
  ところで, bare ところ, and other forms remain outside the verified scope.
