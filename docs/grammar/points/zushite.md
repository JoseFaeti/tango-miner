Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without doing; formal or literary.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verbal negative recognized by 〜ず followed immediately by separate
し (する) and て tokens. The span is ずして and the preceding verb remains
lexical evidence. Tests cover Sudachi verb forms, boundaries, lexical controls,
reservation, card identity and the overlapping 〜ず card.

## Limitations

Other tokenizations and contextual meanings are unverified. A structural
match can occur inside longer constructions, including にあらずして; overlapping
grammar cards can coexist.
