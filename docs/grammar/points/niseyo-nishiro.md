# 〜にせよ / 〜にしろ

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if or whichever.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にせよ and にしろ after verbs, adjectives and auxiliaries,
established expressions such as 何にしろ, どちらにしろ and いずれにせよ,
and repeated alternatives such as 学生にしろ先生にしろ (including mixed forms).
Sentence boundaries block pairing. The preceding expression
remains available as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Isolated nouns are ambiguous and do not match: 皆殺しにしろ and 彼にしろ
remain lexical imperative evidence. This also misses valid isolated nominal
concessives such as 雨にせよ出かける; it is a precision tradeoff, not a general
noun filter. Tests cover the reported command, repeated noun alternatives,
established expressions, verbal clauses and inflections.
Repeated commands can still be ambiguous. Other tokenizations are unverified.
The separate paired construction also has its own card.
