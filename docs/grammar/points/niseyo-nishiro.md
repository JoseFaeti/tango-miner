# 〜にせよ / 〜にしろ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if or whichever.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にせよ and にしろ after a preceding expression, including
nouns, adjectives, clauses and repeated alternatives. The preceding expression
remains available as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Literal commands such as 彼にしろ have the same tokens and may also match.
Other tokenizations are unverified. The separate paired construction is not
identified as its own card.
