- [Meaning](#meaning)

## Meaning

Even more than; more than ever when the comparison is with an earlier time.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にも増して or にもまして, retaining the
comparison target as lexical evidence. Sudachi full dictionary analyzes the
kanji form as 増す + て and the kana form as the adverb まして. Focused tests
cover these forms, boundaries, unrelated uses, reservation and identity. The
kana form also produces the existing 〜まして candidate. The rule cannot always
distinguish comparison from a literal increase or an unrelated adverb; other
spellings, attachments and tokenizations remain unverified. Dictionary
expression precedence may suppress a match when enabled. The separate
[〜にも増して entry](nimomashite-a0338bc3.md) describes the same spelling.
