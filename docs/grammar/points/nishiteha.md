# 〜にしては

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Considering; for a given standard, the result is unexpected.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent `にしては` after a preceding expression, including noun,
adjective, and clause examples. The preceding expression remains lexical
evidence. Overlapping rules can also produce cards, preserving possible literal
readings. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and
card identity.

Literal にする followed by ては can share these tokens, including prohibitions
such as `代表にしてはいけない`; the matcher cannot distinguish these meanings.
Other tokenizations and colloquial contractions are not covered.
