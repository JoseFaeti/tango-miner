# 〜をはじめ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Including; starting with a prominent example.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をはじめ / を始め, including before
とする and として, with the noun and following words outside the span.
Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, inflected verb controls, reservation
and identity. The separate 〜をはじめとして point is not implemented as a
longer span. Literal continuative readings such as 仕事を始め、帰宅した share
the same tokenization and may be matched; semantic disambiguation and other
spellings or tokenizations remain unverified.
