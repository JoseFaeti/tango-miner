# 〜てやまない

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Earnestly and continuously.

## Coverage

Recognizes a verb followed by て/で, やま/止ま/已ま and the negative
auxiliary, including past and conditional forms. Tests cover Sudachi analyses,
literal 止まない, positive やむ and preservation of the preceding verb.
The same pattern can describe literally not stopping an action, so matches are
ambiguous. Other spellings, tokenizations and inflections remain unverified.
