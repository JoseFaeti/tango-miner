Status: [~]

## Meaning

As if to say or convey something through an action or appearance.

## Implemented

Matches verbal irrealis plus んとばかりに, including separate auxiliary,
merged verbal and サ変 noun plus せん analyses. Focused tests cover Sudachi
forms, controls, reservation, lexical verb preservation and card identity.
The separate ん is excluded from the negative ぬ / ん card.

## Limitations

A match beginning inside a merged token cannot reserve that token under the
whole-token reservation mechanism. Context affects interpretation; unusual
tokenizations and corpus precision and recall remain unassessed.
