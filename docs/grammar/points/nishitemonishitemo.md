# 〜にしても〜にしても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Whether ... or ...; the outcome applies to either alternative.

## Coverage and limitations

Recognizes two successive source-aligned にしても markers with an intervening
alternative, including noun and predicate alternatives. Sentence-ending punctuation
blocks pairing; commas are allowed. The first marker is the recorded span, so the
intervening words remain available for lexical mining. The existing single-marker
rule can also match both markers. Three alternatives produce two overlapping pairs.

This is a structural heuristic: unrelated concessive clauses in one sentence may
be paired, and implicit alternatives or other forms are not recognized. Focused
tests cover noun and predicate alternatives, punctuation, single markers, lexical
retention and grammar identity.
