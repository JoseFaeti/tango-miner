# 〜としたところで

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if; even assuming.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent preceding word followed by としたところで or とした所で
  produces a card, reserving the expression while retaining the preceding word.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, controls, identity, reservation and overlap
  with 〜たところで.
- Literal readings can also match; the matcher cannot determine whether the
  phrase is concessive. Other tokenizations and variants remain unverified.
