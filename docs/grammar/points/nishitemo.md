# 〜にしても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even assuming or even if.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent `にしても` after a preceding expression, including noun,
adjective and clause examples. The preceding expression remains lexical evidence;
overlapping rules can also produce cards. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Literal にする followed by ても shares these tokens, so the matcher cannot
distinguish it from the concessive meaning. Other tokenizations and contracted
forms are not covered. Repeated alternatives are not identified as a separate
paired construction.
