# 〜と言わんばかりに

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Implemented](#implemented)
- [Limitations](#limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As if to say something, conveyed through an action or expression.

## Implemented

Matches と言わんばかりに and といわんばかりに with adjacent Sudachi
tokens. Tests cover both spellings, unrelated forms, spacing, card identity,
reservation and overlap with 〜んばかりに. The full phrase is reserved;
the general grammar card can also be emitted for the same occurrence.

## Limitations

Unusual tokenizations and corpus precision and recall remain unassessed.
