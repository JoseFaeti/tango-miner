# 〜というものがある

Status: [~]

## Meaning

There is something called; introduces the existence of a named thing.

## Tracked scope

Matches adjacent `という`, `と言う`, or `っていう`, followed by nominal
`ものが` and positive `ある`, `あった`, `あります`, or `ありました`.
The span starts at the quotation particle. Full-dictionary tests cover these
forms, competing rules, lexical controls, card identity and reservation.
The construction may also describe a literal named object; context is not
used to distinguish readings. Negative and other inflections, alternative
spellings and merged analyses are not covered. Dictionary expression precedence
can suppress grammar occurrences; corpus precision and recall are unmeasured.
