# 〜は別として

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Setting a matter aside; apart from it.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent は別として and はべつとして after an attached expression,
including nominalized clauses. The preceding expression remains lexical evidence;
the construction is reserved for grammar. The topic-particle card may also
match は. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, lexical controls,
reservation and identity.

Literal separation, as in 彼は別としてもいい, is ambiguous and can also
match. Other spellings and tokenizations are not covered. Dictionary expression
precedence can suppress a match when enabled.
