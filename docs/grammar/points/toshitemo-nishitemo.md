# 〜としても / 〜にしても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if.

## Verified coverage

Recognizes adjacent としても and にしても after a preceding expression by
reusing the individual rules. Both the combined card and the corresponding
individual card can be emitted; the preceding expression remains lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, repeated markers,
reservation and identity.

Capacity and attempt readings of としても and literal にする followed by
ても share these tokens and can also match. Other tokenizations and contracted
forms are not covered; paired alternatives are not distinguished by this card.
