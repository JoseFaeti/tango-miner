# 〜に伴い

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Along with or as a consequence of a change; formal connective.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by に伴い
or にともない, including before a comma. The preceding word remains lexical
evidence. Analyzer-based tests cover boundaries, polite and longer forms,
reservation and card identity.

The heuristic can also match accompanying relationships other than change;
other tokenizations and sentence-final or particle-followed uses remain unverified.
