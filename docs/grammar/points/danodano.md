# 〜だの〜だの

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Non-exhaustive listing, often conveying dissatisfaction or dismissal.

## Verified coverage

Matches repeated adjacent `だ` + `の` markers after nominal or predicate
tokens, including noun and adjective examples checked with Sudachi full.
Each successive pair yields its first marker; the listed words remain available
as lexical candidates. Sentence boundaries and spaces within a marker block a
match. Dictionary-expression precedence still applies.

The matcher does not determine the speaker's attitude and may confuse other
repeated `だの` sequences with listing. Verb and other predicate forms,
alternative tokenizations, and corpus precision and recall have not been
systematically validated.
