# 〜はもちろん

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not to mention; of course the first item is included, as well as another.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent はもちろん and は勿論 following an attached expression,
including nominalized clauses. The preceding expression stays available as
lexical evidence; the matched particle and adverb are reserved for grammar.
The topic-particle card may also match the same は. Focused tests cover Sudachi
tokenization, spelling, boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and identity.

The matcher does not verify that a second item is present: a topic followed by
the independent adverb もちろん can also match. Other spellings, tokenizations,
and punctuation-separated forms are not covered. Dictionary expression
precedence can suppress a match when enabled.
