# 〜に替えて

Status: [~]

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

## Meaning

In exchange for; replacing something with the preceding noun.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に替えて, preserving the preceding noun
as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, reservation
and card identity. Literal replacement uses are included; the matcher does not
distinguish exchange from other replacement senses. Other tokenizations and
overlapping dictionary expressions remain unverified. に代えて is tracked
separately.
