# 〜ないとも限らない

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Implementation](#implementation)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

A possibility cannot be ruled out; something might happen or be true.

## Implementation

Matches adjacent negative ない + とも + 限る / かぎる in negative plain,
past, selected continuative and conditional forms, and polite negative forms.
The preceding predicate remains available. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover
verb and adjective-like negatives, inflections, unrelated とも限らない,
とは限らない and positive 限る controls, and card reservation. Context can
still make the reading ambiguous. Literary ぬ and other variants remain
unverified; the broader 〜とも限らない is tracked separately.
