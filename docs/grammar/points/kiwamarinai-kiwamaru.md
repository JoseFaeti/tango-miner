# 〜極まりない / 〜極まる

- [Meaning and coverage](#meaning-and-coverage)
- [Limitations](#limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning and coverage

Expresses an extreme degree, following a na-adjectival stem. Matches
極まりない and its past, continuative, te and conditional forms, including
the split hiragana spelling きわまりない; also matches 極まる, 極まった and
極まって. The stem remains available as lexical evidence. Focused tests use
Sudachi full-dictionary tokenizations and check adjacent boundaries and ordinary
verbal uses of 極まる.

## Limitations

Only stems recognized as 形状詞 or 形状詞可能 are supported; other useful
attachments and tokenizations may be missed. Kana spellings of 極まる and
additional inflections have not been validated. 感激極まる and other noun-plus-
verb uses remain lexical candidates rather than being assigned this grammar.
