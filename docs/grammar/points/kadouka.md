# 〜かどうか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Whether.

## Verified behavior

- Separately tokenized かどうか immediately following a noun, pronoun,
  adjective, adjectival noun, verb or auxiliary produces a card meaning
  “whether or not”. Only かどうか is reserved; the attached word remains
  available as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C,
  affirmative, negative, past and polite predicates, nominal and adjectival
  attachments, boundaries, lexical controls and card evidence.
- The rule requires adjacent complete tokens and does not match an expression
  split by punctuation or whitespace, or どうか alone. Other tokenizations,
  including combined tokens and the spelling 如何, are not verified. The
  surface pattern alone cannot resolve every contextual ambiguity.
