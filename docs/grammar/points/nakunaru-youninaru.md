# 〜なくなる / 〜ようになる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Change of state.

## Verified coverage

Verb-negative なく followed by なる matches cessation, including plain, past,
polite, negative and connective forms. The reserved span starts at なく,
leaving the preceding verb available. Tests cover potential verbs, lexical
なくなる, adjective negatives, separated tokens and incomplete endings.
The existing [〜ようになる](youninaru.md) card handles changes in ability and
habit, including negative verbs, under its own identity.

## Remaining limitations

Other inflections and adjective or noun changes of state are not covered by
the new rule. Lexical and semantic ambiguities depend on analyzer tokenization.
