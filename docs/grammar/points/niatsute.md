# 〜にあって

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In a situation, setting, or time; amid particular circumstances.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にあって, including 有って and 在って
when analyzed as forms of ある. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence;
following は, も, and の are outside the match. Focused tests cover Sudachi
forms, boundaries, meeting (会う), reservation, and identity. The general existence rule also emits a card for the overlapping あって.

Literal existence at a place can also match, and kana あって can represent
other verbs if the analyzer assigns the existence lemma. The rule cannot reliably
separate those readings from the formal expression. Other tokenizations and
spellings remain unverified; にあっては and にあっても have separate longer rules.
