## Verified coverage

Matches noun or pronoun + に限ったことではない, including kana 限る,
事, じゃない, past negatives and polite negative ありません. The preceding
word remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and card identity. Other attachment
forms, inflections and contextual distinctions remain unverified.
