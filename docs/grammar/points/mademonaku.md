# 〜までもなく

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior and limitations](#verified-behavior-and-limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without needing to.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches an adjacent non-past verb followed by までもなく. The span starts
  at まで, retaining the verb as lexical evidence.
- Focused Sudachi tests cover examples, noun, past and negative verb controls,
  spacing, the separate までもない form, reservation and card identity.
  Literal readings can share this sequence; morphology cannot always distinguish
  meaning. Other tokenizations, dictionary precedence, and corpus-wide precision
  and recall remain unverified.
