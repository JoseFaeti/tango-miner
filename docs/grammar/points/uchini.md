# 〜うちに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

While or before change.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent attributive verbs, adjectives and auxiliaries, or a noun followed by
  の, with うちに or 内に produce a card. Only the temporal phrase is reserved,
  preserving attached lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C,
  negative predicates, adjective and nominal forms, controls and card evidence.
- Nominal の内に is ambiguous: 家の内に can mean a physical location. The rule
  does not distinguish temporal and spatial meanings. Bare うちに and そのうちに,
  other tokenizations and variants remain outside the verified scope.
