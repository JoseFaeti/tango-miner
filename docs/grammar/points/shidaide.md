## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by separately tokenized 次第 or しだい
  and the particle で produces a card. The marker and particle are reserved;
  the preceding word remains available. Focused tests cover Sudachi mode C,
  both spellings, card evidence, and lexical and immediate-succession controls.
- では is left to its separate point. Combined tokens such as 結果次第 are
  not split, so those occurrences are missed. Other tokenizations and semantic
  ambiguities remain unverified.
