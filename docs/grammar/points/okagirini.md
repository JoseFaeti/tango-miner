# 〜を限りに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Ending an activity with a particular time or event.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by を限りに or をかぎりに produces
  a grammar card. The expression is reserved while the preceding noun remains
  available as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C,
  both spellings, lexical controls, spacing, reservation and card identity.
- The matcher does not verify that the anchor denotes a time or event or that
  the surrounding clause describes an ending. Literal or otherwise ambiguous
  uses may therefore match; other tokenizations and semantic distinctions remain
  unverified.
