# 〜にかけては

Status: [~]

## Meaning

When it comes to a field or skill, often with an assessment of ability.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にかけては, including 掛けては, retaining
the preceding noun as lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi tests cover field and
skill examples, boundaries, a lexical control, reservation, identity and the
overlap with 〜にかけて. Both grammar cards can be produced for the same span.
Time or area uses followed by は, and literal uses such as 壁にかけては,
are structurally indistinguishable and also match. Other forms remain unverified.
