# 〜に限る

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Best or limited to.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun, pronoun, verb, adjective or auxiliary + に限る,
including kana spelling and polite に限ります. Both recommendations and
restrictions share one card; the preceding content remains lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover analyzer tokenization, boundaries, reservation and identity.
Other inflections and related points such as に限って and に限らず are not
covered. Literal restriction readings also match; the rule does not distinguish
the intended sense from context.
