# 〜を契機に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Taking an event as an opportunity or trigger for a change.

## Implemented coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by を契機に, including the shared prefix
of を契機にして, を契機にする and を契機にした. The example and reserved
span cover を through に; the preceding noun remains a lexical candidate.
Focused tests cover Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization, controls, reservation
and identity. The matcher does not distinguish contextual readings.

Other spellings and tokenizations, and the related を契機として form, have
not been implemented or verified; the point remains partial.
