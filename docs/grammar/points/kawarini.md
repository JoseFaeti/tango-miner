# 〜かわりに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Instead of; in exchange for; contrasting a benefit and drawback.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent attributive verb, adjective or auxiliary + 代わりに / かわりに,
including negative, past and な-adjective forms. The preceding predicate remains
lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi tests cover substitution, compensation,
contrast, spelling, boundaries, reservation and card identity. The form alone
does not distinguish these senses. Noun + の代わりに belongs to the separate
[〜に代わって / 〜の代わりに](nikawatsute-nokawarini.md) card; standalone
代わりに and other attachments remain unverified.
