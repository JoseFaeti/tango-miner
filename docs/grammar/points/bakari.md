# ばかり

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Only, excessive or recent.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent particle ばかり, ばっかり and ばっか after a source token
  produces a card for limitation or excess, including てばかり. Only the
  marker is reserved; preceding words remain available as lexical evidence.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, lexical controls and card identity.
- Verb + たばかり is handled by the separate recent-action card, though it
  can also mean repeated or exclusive activity. Causal ばかりに is excluded.
  The matcher does not disambiguate approximation or other meanings, and
  unrecognized tokenizations and additional variants remain unverified.

- Additive ばかりでなく is handled by its separate card.
