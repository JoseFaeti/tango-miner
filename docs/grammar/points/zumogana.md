# 〜ずもがな

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Implemented behavior](#implemented-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Better left undone; needless to do. 言わずもがな often describes something
better left unsaid.

## Implemented behavior

Matches an irrealis verb followed by ずもがな, including the Sudachi splits
ず / も / がな and ず / もが / な. The span starts at ず, retaining the verb
as lexical evidence. Tests cover both splits, する, surrounding particles,
negative controls, reservation and card examples.

Other tokenizations and corpus precision and recall remain unverified; the
rule does not distinguish contextual interpretations of the construction.
