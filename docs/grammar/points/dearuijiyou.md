# 〜である以上

[Contents](#meaning) · [Verified behavior and limitations](#verified-behavior-and-limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Given that something is the case, a consequence or commitment follows.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches copular である以上 with optional adjacent は, reserving the
  construction while retaining the preceding predicate. The overlapping
  〜である and 〜以上は cards can also be emitted.
- Focused Sudachi split-mode C tests cover noun and adjectival predicates,
  optional は, spacing, unrelated 以上, card identity and reservation.
  Dictionary expression precedence can suppress a match. The matcher does
  not verify the consequence and inherits 〜である's copular ambiguity;
  other tokenizations and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unmeasured.
