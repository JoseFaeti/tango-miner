# 〜を顧みることなく

- [Meaning](#meaning)

## Meaning

Without regard for; disregarding.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を顧みることなく, including kana
をかえりみることなく. The grammar span starts at を; the preceding word
remains lexical evidence. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover both spellings,
pronouns, neighboring forms and boundaries; integration tests cover token
reservation, example surfaces and canonical card identity.

Literal neglect or reflection can share this form and is not filtered.
省みる is not treated as an alternative spelling of 顧みる. Other attachments,
事 spellings and tokenizations remain unsupported or unverified.
