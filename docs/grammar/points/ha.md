# は

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Topic and contrast.

## Verified coverage

Matches a complete Sudachi `は` topic particle (`助詞, 係助詞`) adjacent
to a preceding token, including topic, contrast, and combinations such as
`には`. Only the particle is reserved; the topic and other components remain
available as lexical evidence. Dictionary-expression precedence applies, and
other grammar rules may overlap the particle, including in `ては` forms.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, multiple occurrences, lexical controls,
reservation, examples, and the registered identity. The rule does not distinguish
topic from contrast or other uses of the particle. Standalone and spaced
particles, punctuation other than a closing bracket before the particle, and
forms tokenized as part of a larger token are unsupported. Corpus precision,
recall, and effects on lexical candidates have not been measured.
