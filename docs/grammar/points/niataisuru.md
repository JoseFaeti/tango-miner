# 〜に値する

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Worthy of; deserving of, in formal usage.

## Verified coverage

Matches に値する after a noun or verb, including past, negative, polite,
te and conditional forms, such as 評価に値する and 読むに値した. Sudachi
full splits 値 and する; the rule reserves the construction while preserving
the preceding expression. Focused tests cover inflections, unrelated words,
source boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Literal monetary value, as in 百万円に値する, also matches; the rule cannot
distinguish this from figurative worthiness. Kana spelling にあたいする,
literary に値せず, other inflections and alternative tokenizations remain
unsupported. The attachment check does not establish semantic suitability.
