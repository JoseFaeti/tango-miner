# 〜ず

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Negative continuative.

## Verified coverage

Matches separately tokenized verbal ず following an irrealis stem, including
godan, ichidan, する, 来る and a passive auxiliary example. The grammar span
is ず; the verb remains vocabulary. Tests cover lexical and spacing controls,
reservation, card examples and overlap with 〜ずに. The structural match can
also record this card within longer expressions.

## Limitations

Other tokenizations and auxiliary chains have not been assessed. Structural
matching does not distinguish literal negatives from fixed expressions.
Dictionary-expression precedence can suppress a shorter grammar occurrence.
