# 〜に限って言えば

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Speaking only of a particular subject or case.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に限って言えば, including kana spellings.
The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer
tokenization, boundaries, reservation, overlap with に限って and identity.
The shorter に限って card is also retained for the same occurrence.
Other forms and tokenizations are not covered; literal uses can also match.
