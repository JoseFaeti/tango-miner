# 〜に難くない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not difficult to imagine or infer, in a formal or literary expression.

## Implemented behavior

Matches a noun or verb followed by に難くない or にかたくない, including
なかった, なく, なければ and an optional です. Sudachi full-dictionary
tests cover 想像に難くない, 想像にかたくない and 推察するに難くない;
the card reserves the expression while retaining the preceding lexical candidate.
The spelling 〜にかたくない is covered by the same card. The full registry
also collects the overlapping particle に. Tests cover なく and なければ.
硬くない and non-particle に are controls.

The kana spelling is ambiguous with かたい meaning hard; matching uses the
surrounding construction but cannot resolve meaning. Other inflections and
tokenizations remain unverified. Corpus precision and recall remain unmeasured.
