# 〜というと / 〜といえば

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Speaking of; when it comes to.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent というと and と言うと after a noun, pronoun, adjective or
verb, retaining the preceding topic as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover
Sudachi forms, reservation, identity and controls. The existing
[といったら / といえば](toitsutara-toieba.md) card retains といえば to avoid
duplicate cards for the same occurrence. Literal conditional quotations can
also match; meaning is not disambiguated. Other tokenizations and colloquial
variants remain unverified.
