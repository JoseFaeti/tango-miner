# 〜ながらも

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Concession.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- An adjacent verb continuative, noun, 形状詞 or adjective followed by
  Sudachi's 接続助詞 ながら and 係助詞 も matches when a following clause
  begins before sentence-ending punctuation. The card spans ながらも and
  retains the attached word as lexical evidence. It takes precedence over
  the shorter 〜ながら card. Dictionary expression precedence can suppress it.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C forms, lexical controls, incomplete
  clauses, overlapping cards and token reservation. Other tokenizations and
  corpus-wide precision, recall and expression precedence remain unmeasured.
  Morphology alone cannot verify that the following clause is concessive.
