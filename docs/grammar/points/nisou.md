# 〜に沿う

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Follow or conform to.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に沿う and に沿った, including literal
paths and figurative conformity, in predicate or attributive use. The preceding
noun remains lexical evidence. Tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries,
reservation and card identity. The separate 〜に沿って card handles the
connective. Kana そう may be analyzed as an adverb, as in 道にそう, and is
then missed. Other inflections and alternative tokenizations remain unverified.
