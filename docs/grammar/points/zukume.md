# 〜ずくめ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Entirely consisting of, or nothing but, the named thing.

## Verified behavior

- Matches an adjacent noun followed by the nominal suffix ずくめ or 尽くめ,
  including attributive の and predicate forms. Only the suffix is reserved;
  the noun remains lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenizations, boundaries, card identity and
  unrelated forms. Other tokenizations and meanings remain unverified; the
  matcher does not assess the semantic relationship to the noun.
