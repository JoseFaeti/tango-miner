# 〜と思うと

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

When thinking of.

## Verified behavior

- A finite verb, adjective or auxiliary followed by と思うと or とおもうと
  produces a card for the expression, leaving the preceding predicate available.
  The overlapping 〜と思う card is suppressed for this conditional form.
- Sudachi split mode C tests cover affirmative and negative predicates, spelling,
  reservation and nearby controls. Literal conditional thoughts can also match;
  the rule does not infer the relationship between clauses. Other attachments and
  tokenizations remain unverified.
