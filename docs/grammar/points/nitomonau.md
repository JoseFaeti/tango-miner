# 〜に伴う

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Associated with or accompanying an event or change; attributive form.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by に伴う
or にともなう and a noun. Only に伴う is reserved; the surrounding words remain
lexical evidence. Sudachi full dictionary tests cover both spellings, verb and
noun attachment, boundaries, inflected and connective controls, and card identity.

The heuristic does not distinguish change from other accompanying relationships.
Sentence-final uses and past, negative or polite inflections are outside this
rule's verified coverage. Related connective forms have separate rules for
[〜に伴い](nitomonai.md) and [〜に伴って](nitomonatsute.md).
