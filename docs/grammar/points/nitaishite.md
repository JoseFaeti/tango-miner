# 〜に対して

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Toward, regarding, or in contrast to.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に対して and polite に対しまして,
including に対しては. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation, and identity. The matcher
does not distinguish grammatical uses from literal uses of 対する. Other
tokenizations and attachments remain unverified; に対し and に対する are
