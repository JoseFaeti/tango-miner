# 〜ようと思う

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Intention.

## Coverage

An adjacent Sudachi volitional verb followed by と and 思う matches the same
plain, past, polite, negative and connective forms of 思う as 〜と思う.
The span starts at と because the volitional ending is part of the verb token;
the verb remains lexical. The generic 〜と思う rule excludes these verb
volitionals, so the two cards do not duplicate the occurrence. Connective
と思って can overlap 〜ている. Focused analyzer tests cover godan, ichidan
and irregular verbs, ordinary quotations and nonadjacent forms. Other
tokenizations and inflections, expression precedence, corpus effects and
independent precision and recall remain unevaluated.
