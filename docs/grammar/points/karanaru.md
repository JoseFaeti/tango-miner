# 〜からなる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Consist of.

## Verified coverage and limitations

Matches adjacent noun, pronoun or suffix + からなる, からなった and
からなって, including 成る spelling. The preceding component stays outside
the grammar span; the て form can overlap other grammar cards. Focused tests
cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and
identity. A starting point followed by なる can be ambiguous, and other
inflections, tokenizations and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unverified.
