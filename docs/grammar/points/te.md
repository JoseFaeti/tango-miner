# 〜て

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Connection, sequence and request.

## Implemented coverage

Matches source-aligned connective particles て and で immediately after a
verb, adjective, or auxiliary in a continuative form, including sentence-final
requests and occurrences within longer constructions such as 〜ている. The
example span is the particle; lexical candidates remain available. The rule
does not distinguish connection, sequence, cause, or request by context.

Focused tests cover Sudachi forms 食べて, 読んで, 早くて, requests,
overlap with 〜ている, and noun and case-particle controls. Copular で,
contracted or merged forms, and other tokenizations are not covered.
