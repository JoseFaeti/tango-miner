# 〜まいか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Negative conjectural question or tentative suggestion.

## Tracked scope

Recognizes adjacent verbal まい + sentence-final か using the attachment
checks for [〜まい](mai.md). The card highlights まいか and keeps the
preceding verb available as lexical evidence. The generic 〜まい card can
also be collected. The more specific [〜ではあるまいか](dehaarumaika.md)
owns its recognized overlaps instead of this card. Dictionary expression
precedence can suppress a match.

Focused tests cover Sudachi forms including 行く, 食べ and 来る, lexical and
spacing controls, the specific overlap, reservation and card examples.
The rule does not distinguish conjecture from suggestion in context. Other
segmentations, including noun analyses of すまい and しまい, and independent
corpus precision and recall remain unassessed.
