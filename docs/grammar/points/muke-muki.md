# 〜向け / 〜向き

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Intended for or suited to.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + nominal suffix 向け or 向き, including
suffix-tokenized kana forms. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence;
the suffix is reserved. 向け indicates an intended audience or purpose,
whereas 向き indicates suitability. Suffix 向き also produces the existing
〜に向いている / 〜向き card: the two registered rules retain their identities
and both collect the occurrence. Focused tests cover source spans, reservation,
identity, overlap, and Sudachi forms and controls.

Sudachi can tokenize kana むけ as a verb or a whole compound as one noun;
those forms are not matched. Directional meanings in separately tokenized
suffixes cannot always be distinguished from suitability. Other tokenizations
and inflected or broader constructions remain unverified.
