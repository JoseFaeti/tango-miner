## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に限っては, including kana spelling,
and retains the preceding noun as lexical evidence. The shorter 〜に限って
card can also be emitted for the same occurrence. Context is not used to
distinguish restrictive, contrastive, or literal readings. Other tokenizations
are not covered. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation,
identity, overlap and lexical controls.
