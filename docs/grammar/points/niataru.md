## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent に当たる and にあたる after a noun or pronoun, including
tested past, polite and conditional forms. The preceding expression remains
lexical evidence. Focused tests cover source spans, controls, reservation and
identity. Negative and occasion forms have separate rules.

Focused lexical controls exclude 本当に当たる and impact complements 頭, 壁,
攻撃, 弾 and 球. Pronoun complements followed by conditional と and a nearby
痛手 or 怪我/けが consequence are also excluded, stopping at punctuation.
The three reported source sentences are regression controls. Tests retain
犯罪, calendar, monetary, kinship and ambiguous pronoun correspondence uses.
Rejected occurrences remain available as vocabulary rather than reserving 当たる.

This is a bounded heuristic, not semantic disambiguation: other literal uses
can still match, and the excluded complements may have figurative correspondence
readings. Unknown noun complements and pronouns remain eligible to preserve
recall. Other inflections and tokenizations remain unverified.
