# 〜としても〜としても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Whether ... or ...; the outcome applies to either alternative.

## Coverage and limitations

Recognizes successive source-aligned としても markers with an intervening
alternative, including noun and predicate alternatives. Sentence-ending punctuation
blocks pairing; commas are allowed. The first marker is the recorded span, leaving
the intervening words available for lexical mining. The single-marker rules can
also match both markers. Three alternatives produce two overlapping pairs.

This structural heuristic can pair unrelated concessive clauses or ambiguous
capacity and attempt readings in one sentence. Implicit alternatives and other
tokenizations are not recognized. Focused tests cover tokenization, punctuation,
single markers, lexical retention and grammar identity.
