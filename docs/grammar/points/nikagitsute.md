# 〜に限って

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Only in a particular case, or particularly when the outcome is unexpected.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に限って, including kana spelling. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Both restrictive and unexpected-case
readings share one card; context is not used to distinguish them. The same form
can also express confidence that someone would not do something. Literal uses
of 限る in this form can match as well. Other forms and tokenizations are not
covered. Focused tests cover analyzer tokenization, boundaries, reservation,
identity and related lexical controls.
