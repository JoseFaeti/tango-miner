# 〜ばかりでなく

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not only; as well as.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent ばかりでなく, ばかりではなく and ばかりじゃなく after a
source-aligned preceding token. The preceding word remains lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, neighboring forms, boundaries,
reservation, overlap with 〜ばかり and card identity. The rule does not require
a following も or distinguish contextual senses. Adjacent なくなる is excluded;
other variants and analyzer tokenizations remain unverified.
