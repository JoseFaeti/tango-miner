# 〜ものとする

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Formal stipulation (shall) or assumption.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent attributive verb, adjective or auxiliary followed by
ものとする, including past and polite forms. The predicate and もの remain
available as lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi and integration tests cover
forms, boundaries, components and card identity.

Literal things modified by a predicate can share this tokenization and are
also matched; meaning is not disambiguated. Kanji 物, negative and other
inflections are not covered. 〜ものとして is tracked separately.
