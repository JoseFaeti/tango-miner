# 〜をもって

Status: [~]

## Meaning

With or as of.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をもって, を持って, を以て, and polite
をもちまして / を持ちまして. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence.
Sudachi full-dictionary examples cover a means, an ending date, the 以 spelling,
and the polite form; focused tests cover boundaries, reservation and identity.
Literal possession or carrying, such as 本をもっている and 鞄を持って帰る,
is structurally indistinguishable and also matches. Other attachments and forms
remain unverified. Longer expressions beginning をもって, including separately
scheduled points, may also match this prefix.
