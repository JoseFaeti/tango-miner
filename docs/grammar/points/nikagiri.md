# 〜に限り

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Only; formal.

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に限り, including kana spelling, as a
formal restriction. The preceding expression remains lexical evidence.

and card identity are also tested. Polite に限ります belongs to 〜に限る.
