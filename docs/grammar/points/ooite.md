# 〜をおいて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Apart from; other than, often identifying the only suitable person or thing.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をおいて / を置いて followed immediately
by ほか, 他 or 外. The noun and following word remain lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation and identity.
Literal placement followed by ほか can still match. Uses without an immediately
following ほか and other spellings or tokenizations remain unverified.
