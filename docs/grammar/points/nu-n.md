# 〜ぬ / 〜ん

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Literary or old-fashioned ぬ and colloquial or dialectal ん express verbal negation.

## Supported behavior

Matches a separate Sudachi negative auxiliary ぬ or ん following a verbal
irrealis, including passive and causative auxiliaries. Also accepts the
continuative analysis of ichidan verbs and 来る, whose stems coincide with
the irrealis in examples such as 食べぬ and 来ん. Only the auxiliary is
reserved, leaving the verb available as vocabulary. Focused tests cover these
forms, lexical 死ぬ, explanatory ん, polite ません, spacing, and card
reservation.

## Limitations

Sudachi may analyze せん as a noun, and dialectal past んかった as ない;
these are not matched. Classical perfective ぬ after an identical ichidan
continuative stem is formally ambiguous and may be counted as negative.
Other tokenizations and inflected variants remain unverified.
