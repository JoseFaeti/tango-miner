# 〜を抜きにして

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を抜きにして, including ぬき. The omitted
item remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries,
reservation and identity. The prefix also matches を抜きにしては; a separate
longer rule emits 〜を抜きにしては as its own candidate. Literal-purpose
readings such as 歯を抜きにして病院へ行く are ambiguous in this tokenization.
Other inflections, constructions without を and other tokenizations remain
unverified.
