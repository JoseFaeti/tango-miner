# 〜のいかんにかかわらず

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Regardless of the outcome or circumstances of the preceding noun.

## Verified behavior

- After a noun or pronoun, recognizes のいかん／の如何にかかわらず,
  including 関わらず and 拘わらず. The noun remains available for lexical
  processing; の and the remainder are reserved for grammar. The overlapping
  〜いかんにかかわらず and 〜にかかわらず cards are also collected.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, unrelated expressions,
  reservation and identity. Other segmentations and contextual ambiguities
  remain unverified; the surface pattern cannot establish the reading of 如何.
