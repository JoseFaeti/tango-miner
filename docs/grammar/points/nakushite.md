Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without; formal or literary.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by separate なく (ない adjective),
し (する) and て tokens. The span is なくして; the noun remains lexical
evidence. Tests cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, the lexical verb なくす,
reservation and card identity.

## Limitations

Other tokenizations and contextual meanings are unverified. The structural
match also occurs within 〜なくしては; that separate point is unimplemented.
