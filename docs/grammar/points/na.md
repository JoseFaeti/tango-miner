# な

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Self-directed or prohibitive sentence ending.

## Verified behavior

Matches Sudachi final-particle な and なあ adjacent to a verb, adjective,
adjectival noun, auxiliary, noun, pronoun or adverb, including 行くな、行くなよ、
綺麗だなあ and embedded そうだなと思う. Only the particle is reserved;
the preceding word remains available as a lexical candidate. Tests cover these
forms, copular 静かな町, lexical かなしい, spacing, and overlap with 〜かな.

## Limitations

The same form can express reflection, prohibition or other pragmatic meanings;
the rule does not distinguish them. It relies on Sudachi's final-particle
analysis and excludes particle combinations such as かな, handled separately.
Other tokenizations and elongated spellings remain unverified.
