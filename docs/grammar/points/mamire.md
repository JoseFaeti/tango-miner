# 〜まみれ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Covered or smeared with something, often an undesirable substance.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent noun + まみれ when Sudachi identifies まみれ as a
  名詞的 suffix, including attributive and predicate contexts. Only the
  suffix is reserved, leaving the noun available as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenizations, boundaries, card identity and
  reservation, and the separate verb まみれる. The matcher does not judge
  whether the noun denotes a substance or whether the context is negative.
- Sudachi often tokenizes 泥まみれ, 血まみれ and kanji spellings such as
  血塗れ as whole words. These remain lexical candidates and are not matched;
  other tokenizations and forms remain unverified.
