# 〜ものがある

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Expresses a noteworthy quality or an impression evoked by something.

## Coverage and limitations

Matches an attributive verb, i-adjective or auxiliary followed by ものがある,
ものがあった, ものがあります or ものがありました. The modifier remains
available as vocabulary. Sudachi full-dictionary examples and reservation are
covered in `src/grammar_rules/rules/mono_ga_aru/test_index.py`.

Literal descriptions such as 机に古いものがある have the same structure and
may be detected; context is needed to distinguish them. Negative and other
inflections, spelling variants and other tokenizations remain unverified.
