# か

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Question or uncertainty.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent, complete Sudachi sentence-final `か` particle. The
particle alone is reserved, preserving neighboring lexical evidence; more
specific rules such as `のか` can also match. Focused tests cover direct
questions, lexical and spacing controls, reservation, overlap, and identity.

Embedded questions and alternatives tagged as other particle types, isolated
or spaced particles, and particles following punctuation are not supported.
The rule does not distinguish questions from other uses of sentence-final
`か`. Corpus precision, recall, and lexical effects have not been measured.
