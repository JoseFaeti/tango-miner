# 〜に即して

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In accordance with.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に即して, including kana spelling when
recognized as 即する or 即す. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests
cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, reservation and identity. Attributive に即した has a separate rule; other inflections
and tokenizations are not covered.
