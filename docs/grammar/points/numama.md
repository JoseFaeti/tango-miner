# 〜ぬまま

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without doing; remaining in a negative state, with a literary negative.

## Implemented

The rule matches a separate verbal negative auxiliary ぬ followed immediately
by the noun まま or 儘, retaining the preceding verb as component evidence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, lexical controls, reservation, identity and
overlap with 〜まま and 〜ぬ / 〜ん.

## Limitations

Merged and unusual spellings, analyzer errors, and corpus-level precision and
recall remain unassessed. Context may affect the interpretation.
