- [Meaning](#meaning)

## Meaning

In response to; to meet a request or expectation.

## Implementation

Kana spelling of [〜に応えて](nikotaete.md), implemented by the existing
`nikotaete` rule with the shared stable card identity 〜に応えて. See that
specification for canonical coverage and limitations; no separate card is emitted.
Focused tests verify noun/pronoun + にこたえて and polite にこたえまして,
exclude finite/attributive forms and broken boundaries, and verify shared card
examples and noun preservation across kana and kanji spellings.
Semantic ambiguity and other tokenizations remain as documented there.
