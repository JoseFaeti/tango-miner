# 〜までのことだ

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior and limitations](#verified-behavior-and-limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Only have to.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches an adjacent non-past verb followed by までのことだ or までのことです,
  including 事 spelling. The card spans まで through the copula and retains
  the verb as lexical evidence.
- Focused Sudachi tests cover these forms, time and place noun controls,
  spacing, unrelated endings, card identity and reservation. Literal temporal
  readings after a verb can share this sequence and cannot be distinguished
  reliably. Other inflections and tokenizations, dictionary precedence, and
  corpus-wide precision and recall remain unverified.
