# 〜ざるを得ない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot avoid doing; have no choice but to do.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb followed directly by ざるを得ない (also えない), including
past, continuative, conditional, ず / ぬ and negative polite forms. The
preceding verb remains available as vocabulary; the grammar span starts at
ざる. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover 行か, 食べ, せ and 来, and reject
standalone ざる, positive 得た, gaps and incomplete negatives. Reservation and
card examples are tested.
