# 〜の至り

[Meaning](#meaning) | [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

The utmost of a feeling or state, or a consequence of a quality, as in
若気の至り (a consequence of youthful inexperience).

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + の至り when 至り is analyzed as a noun,
retaining the preceding word as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer
forms, boundaries, verb controls, reservation and card identity. Kana いたり
is supported when analyzed as the noun, but Sudachi does not do so in the tested
感激のいたり example. Sudachi treats 若気の至り as one token, so the rule
does not match it; the compound remains available for lexical mining. Literal
uses are not distinguished from the grammar meaning, and other tokenizations
remain unverified.
