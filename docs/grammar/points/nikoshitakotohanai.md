# 〜に越したことはない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Nothing is better than the preceding action or state; it is best to do so.

## Coverage and limitations

Matches adjacent `に越したことはない` after a verb, adjective, auxiliary or
noun, including negative predicates and the supported past and polite negative
endings. The card reserves the construction itself, leaving the preceding
predicate available as lexical evidence. Split Sudachi full-dictionary forms
are covered by focused tests.

The identical sequence can also describe literally crossing or moving to a
place, especially after a noun; the matcher cannot distinguish those readings.
Other spellings and tokenizations are not yet verified. The negative-predicate
variant also has a separate card.
