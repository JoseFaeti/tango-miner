# 〜そばから

[Contents](../README.md)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Repeatedly as soon as an action occurs, often with an undesirable outcome.

## Coverage

Matches そばから or 側から immediately after a prenominal verb or a verb
followed by past た/だ. The card captures the marker and retains the verb.
Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover non-past and past forms and noun controls.
The matcher does not establish repetition or distinguish every literal spatial
reading following a verb; these ambiguous occurrences require review. Other
tokenizations and auxiliary chains remain unverified.
