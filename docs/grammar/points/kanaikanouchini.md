# 〜か〜ないかのうちに

[Meaning](#meaning) | [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Almost as soon as an event happened, another followed.

## Coverage

Matches a verb followed by か, the same verb in the negative, and かのうちに
or かの内に. The first verb may be past tense. Sudachi-based tests cover
godan, ichidan, する and 来る, along with different-verb and boundary controls.
The card reserves the span beginning with the first か, including the repeated
verb; the first verb remains available as lexical evidence. Other grammar rules
may also report overlapping occurrences.

Compound and auxiliary inflections, alternative tokenizations and omitted or
altered repetitions have not been established; corpus precision and recall
remain unmeasured.
