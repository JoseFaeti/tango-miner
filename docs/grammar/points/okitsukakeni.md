# 〜をきっかけに

## Implemented coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by をきっかけに or を切っ掛けに,
including the shared prefix of をきっかけにして, をきっかけにする and
をきっかけにした. The example and reserved span cover を through に;
the preceding noun remains available as a lexical candidate. Focused tests
cover Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization, controls, reservation and identity.

The rule does not distinguish contextual readings of the sequence. Other
spellings and tokenizations, and the related をきっかけとして form, have not
been implemented or verified; the point remains partial.
