# 〜について

Status: [~]

## Meaning

About; concerning a topic.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + について and polite につきまして,
including についての and については. The preceding word remains lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation,
and identity. Kanji 付いて is excluded to preserve literal attachment;
kana ついて is ambiguous and literal uses can still match. Other tokenizations
and attachments remain unverified.
