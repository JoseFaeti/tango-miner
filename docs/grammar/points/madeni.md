# 〜までに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Deadline.

## Verified behavior

- Separately tokenized までに after an adjacent noun, pronoun, verb or auxiliary
  produces a card, reserving only までに and retaining the attached word.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, nominal and verbal deadlines,
  boundaries, controls and card evidence.
- This surface pattern also matches non-deadline uses, including spatial endpoints
  such as 駅までに; it does not determine meaning from context. Combined tokens
  such as 今まで followed by に are not matched, preserving the lexical token.
  Other tokenizations and attachments remain unverified.
- With observed Sudachi tokens, これほどまでに and それほどまでに are not
  matched because ほど is a particle. Locative demonstratives such as ここまでに
  immediately followed by an adjective are excluded as potential degree uses;
  this can also miss a deadline interpretation. Other degree uses remain ambiguous.
