# 〜に伴って

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Accompanying change.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by
に伴って or にともなって. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity.
Other accompanying relationships can also match; the pattern does not
distinguish change from other meanings. The shorter に伴い and attributive
に伴う forms and other tokenizations are not supported.
