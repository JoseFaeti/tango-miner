# 〜を限りにして

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Ending an activity with a particular time or event.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by を限りにして or をかぎりにして
  produces a grammar card. The anchor remains available as lexical evidence;
  the shorter 〜を限りに rule can also match. Focused tests cover Sudachi
  split-mode C, both spellings, controls, spacing, reservation and card identity.
- The matcher does not verify the anchor's meaning or whether the clause
  describes an ending. Literal and ambiguous uses may match. Other
  tokenizations and related inflections remain unverified.
