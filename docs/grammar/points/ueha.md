# 〜うえは

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Now that; since a situation has arisen, a consequence or resolve follows.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches separately tokenized 上は and うえは after an adjacent attributive
  verb or a verb followed by past た or だ. Only 上は is reserved; the
  preceding predicate remains available as lexical evidence.
- Focused Sudachi split-mode C tests cover these forms, literal locations,
  spacing, card evidence and reservation. The matcher does not check the
  following consequence, and predicate-attached literal uses may also match.
  Other tokenizations, dictionary expression precedence, and corpus-wide
  precision and recall remain unverified.
