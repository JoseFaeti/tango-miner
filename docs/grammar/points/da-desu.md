# だ / です

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Copula and politeness.

## Verified behavior

- Matches source-adjacent auxiliary tokens だ and です in their finite present
  forms, including polite です after an adjective. Only the auxiliary is
  reserved, retaining the preceding lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover nominal and adjectival predicates, past and negative
  controls, whitespace, lexical and part-of-speech controls, reservation,
  card examples and identity registration.
- Past, negative, conditional and other inflected forms are outside this rule;
  some are separately scheduled points. Merged tokenizations are not recovered.
  Overlap with longer grammar and dictionary expressions depends on pipeline
  precedence. Precision and recall have not been independently evaluated.
