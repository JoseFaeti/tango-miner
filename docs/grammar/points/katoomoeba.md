# 〜かと思えば

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Then unexpectedly.

## Verified behavior

- A past verb immediately followed by かと思えば or かとおもえば produces
  one card. Only the expression is reserved, leaving the verb as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split mode C, punctuation, lexical controls and
  reservation. Literal conditional thoughts with the same sequence may match;
  the rule does not infer the relationship between clauses. Present-tense
  alternating situations share this card; see the [separate scope](katoomoeba-5d9c4546.md).
  Other attachments and tokenizations remain unverified.
