# 〜といった

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Introduces examples of a category: such as, or like.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent といった or と言った before a category noun, retaining
  the preceding example and following noun as lexical evidence. Tests retain
  single examples, lists, and などといった.
- Excludes the heads ところ / 所 and 方 / ほう: these signal assessment,
  place, or manner rather than an example category. The separate
  [approximation rule](toitsutatokoroda.md) covers といったところかしら.
  特殊な合金といった方が近いでしょうか retains literal 言う evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, card identity, reservation and
  nearby literal and conditional forms, including both reported sentences.
- This is a bounded noun-head distinction, not general semantic inference.
  Other literal quotations modifying nouns remain ambiguous; genuine example
  categories expressed with 所 or 方 are also excluded. Other variants and
  tokenizations remain unverified.
