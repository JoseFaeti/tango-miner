# 〜といった

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Introduces examples of a category: such as, or like.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent といった or と言った before a noun, retaining the
  preceding example and following noun as lexical evidence. The complete
  といったところだ estimate is handled by its separate rule.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, card identity, reservation and
  nearby literal and conditional forms. A literal quotation modifying a noun
  can have the same form and is not distinguished; other variants and
  tokenizations remain unverified.
