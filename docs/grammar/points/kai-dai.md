# 〜かい / 〜だい

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Informal question.

## Verified behavior

Matches adjacent Sudachi final-particle か + い and copular だ + final-particle
い, including 行くかい, 元気かい, 何だい and 行くんだい. The card span
contains the ending; its tokens are reserved while the attached word remains
available. Tests cover these forms, lexical 貝, 何回 and ちょうだい, spacing,
and かいな, which belongs to a separate point.

## Limitations

Sudachi's analysis determines coverage. The matcher cannot distinguish an
assertive だい from a question, or reliably establish the pragmatic meaning
of かい. Other tokenizations and elongated forms remain unverified.
