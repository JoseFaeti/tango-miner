# 〜たばかり

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Just did; the speaker considers the action recent.

## Verified behavior

- A verb followed by past た or だ and the adjacent particle ばかり,
  ばっかり, or ばっか produces a card, including before a copula or の.
  The marker alone is reserved; the verb and past auxiliary remain available
  as lexical evidence. Focused tests use Sudachi full dictionary tokenization
  and check card identity, source examples, reservation, and lexical controls.
- たばかり can also mean that someone only or repeatedly did an action;
  the rule cannot distinguish this reading from recency. It excludes ばかりに,
  which can express cause, and does not cover negative past forms or other
  variants. Further source evidence is needed to assess those cases.
