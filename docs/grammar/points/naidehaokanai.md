# 〜ないではおかない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Inevitably causes something to happen, or is determined not to leave an
action undone.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verbal negative followed by ではおかない or では置かない,
including past, negative polite, continuative and conditional forms with
separate Sudachi tokens. The preceding verb remains vocabulary; the grammar
span starts at ない. Tests cover causative verbs, incomplete or positive
endings, gaps, adjective and lexical controls, reservation and card examples.

## Limitations

The same sequence can have a literal reading, which token matching cannot
distinguish. Other segmentations, contractions and inflections have not been
assessed.
