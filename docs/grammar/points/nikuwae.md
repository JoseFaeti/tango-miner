Status: [~]

## Meaning

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に加え, including kana spelling. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms,
boundaries, reservation and identity; に加えて and に加えまして retain their
separate identity. Literal additions can also match. Other attachments and
forms remain unverified.
