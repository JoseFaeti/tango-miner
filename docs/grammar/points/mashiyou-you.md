# 〜ましょう / 〜よう

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Volition and invitation.

## Coverage

Recognizes Sudachi verb tokens marked as volitional, including 読もう,
食べよう, 来よう and しよう, and adjacent verb continuative forms followed
by ましょう or colloquial ましょ. The plain form spans the whole verb;
the polite form spans the suffix. Plain volitional verbs remain available for
lexical mining, while the polite suffix can be reserved. The card can overlap
with 〜ようと思う and 〜ようとする. Focused analyzer and boundary tests
cover these forms and unrelated よう and ordinary verb forms.

The inflection can express conjecture or appear in other constructions, so
form alone does not establish an invitation or intention. Other tokenizations,
corpus precision, recall and expression precedence remain unevaluated.
