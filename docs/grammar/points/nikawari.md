# 〜に代わり

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In place of or on behalf of; formal.

## Coverage

Matches noun or pronoun + に代わり / にかわり as a connective, including
before a comma. Sudachi's verb analysis distinguishes the noun 代わり and
変わる; polite 代わります is excluded. Analyzer-based examples and token
reservation are covered by `nikawari/test_index.py`.

This is a surface and tokenization heuristic: lexical substitution and
representation cannot always be distinguished, and other spellings and
analyzer segmentations have not been verified.
