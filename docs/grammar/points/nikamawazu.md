# 〜にかまわず

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Regardless of; without regard for.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にかまわず and に構わず after a source token, including
noun and nominalized-clause examples. A following に is allowed; the card span
ends at ず. The preceding token remains available as lexical evidence.
Focused Sudachi full-dictionary tests verify both spellings, source boundaries,
negative controls and the neighboring にかかわらず construction. Integration
tests verify reservation, example surface and canonical card identity.

Other spellings and analyzer tokenizations remain unverified. Contextual
readings of literal 構う are not distinguished, and no following clause is
required. This rule does not cover にもかまわず or にはかまわず.
