# 〜ところに / 〜ところへ

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Just when.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent attributive verb, or an attributive auxiliary chain attached
  to a verb, followed by ところ/所 and に/へ produces one
  shared card. The marker and particle are reserved; the preceding predicate
  remains available. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, lexical controls,
  card integration, and overlap with 〜たところ.
- Adjective and noun modifiers are excluded, including temporal uses such as
  忙しいところに. Literal locations after verbs such as 昨日行った所へ still
  match; semantic disambiguation and other forms remain unverified.
