# 〜ところに / 〜ところへ

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Just when.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent attributive verb or adjective, or an attributive auxiliary
  attached to a predicate, followed by ところ/所 and に/へ produces one
  shared card. The marker and particle are reserved; the preceding predicate
  remains available. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, lexical controls,
  card integration, and overlap with 〜たところ.
- Literal locations such as 昨日行った所へ also match. The rule does not
  distinguish a moment or situation from a place; other forms and semantic
  disambiguation remain unverified.
