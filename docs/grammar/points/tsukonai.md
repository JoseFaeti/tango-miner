# 〜っこない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot possibly.

## Implemented coverage

Matches a verb continuative stem followed by Sudachi's っこ suffix and adjectival
ない, expressing that the action cannot possibly happen. The card span starts
at っこ and includes ない, なかった, なくて, なければ, or ないです when
tokenized as expected. Verb and suffix must be adjacent; noun uses such as
鬼ごっこ are excluded. The verb remains available as a lexical candidate.

Focused tests exercise Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization, past and connective
forms, noun and ordinary negative controls, incomplete forms, and card creation.
Other contractions and analyzer segmentations remain unverified.
