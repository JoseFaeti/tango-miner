# 〜に反して

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Contrary to; against.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に反して and polite に反しまして,
including に反しては. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation, and identity. Literal
uses of 反する are not distinguished; other tokenizations and attachments
remain unverified. に反し and に反する are separate tracked points.
