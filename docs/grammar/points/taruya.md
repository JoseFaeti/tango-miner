# 〜たるや

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As for; speaking of (an emphatic literary topic).

## Verified coverage

Matches nominal expressions followed by auxiliary たる and particle や,
including 怒りたるや and nominalized adjectives such as 美しさたるや.
Only たるや is reserved. Sudachi full tokenizations are covered alongside
lexical 足る and 樽 and the attributive construction 教師たる者.
Alternative analyzer segmentations and semantic ambiguity remain unverified.
