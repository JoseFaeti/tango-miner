# 〜ことに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Introduces the speaker's reaction to the following situation: “to my delight”,
“unfortunately”, or “to my surprise”.

## Implemented coverage

Matches ことに and 事に after selected reaction adjectives (嬉しいことに),
な-adjectives (残念なことに), and past reaction verbs (驚いたことに).
The card highlights ことに; the predicate remains available as lexical evidence.
Focused tests use Sudachi full tokenization and cover ordinary nominalizations,
ことにする, ことになる, and card integration.

## Limitations

The reaction vocabulary is deliberately incomplete, so other predicates and
inflections are missed. Literal references to a happy or surprising thing can
still match; punctuation alone cannot distinguish those from speaker reactions.
