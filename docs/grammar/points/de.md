# で

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Place, means and cause.

## Verified coverage

Matches a complete Sudachi `で` case particle (`助詞, 格助詞`) adjacent to a
preceding token, including place, means and cause uses. Only the particle is
reserved, leaving neighboring words as lexical evidence. Dictionary-expression
precedence and overlapping grammar rules continue to apply.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, copula and verb-conjunction controls,
boundaries, reservation, examples and identity. The rule does not distinguish
senses and may also match other case-particle uses. Standalone and spaced
particles, punctuation other than a closing bracket before the particle, and
forms tokenized within a larger token are unsupported. Corpus precision, recall
and effects on lexical candidates have not been measured.
