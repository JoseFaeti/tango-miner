# 〜にしたところで

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if; even assuming.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent preceding word followed by にしたところで or にした所で
  produces a card, reserving the expression while retaining the preceding word.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, controls, identity, reservation and overlap
  with 〜たところで.
- Literal readings such as a place where something was made can also match;
  the matcher cannot determine whether the phrase is concessive. Other
  tokenizations and variants remain unverified.
