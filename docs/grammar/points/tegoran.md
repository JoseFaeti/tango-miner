# 〜てごらん

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An informal suggestion or instruction to try doing something; ごらんなさい and
ごらんください are more polite variants.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb or auxiliary continuative form followed by connective て/で and
ごらん, ご覧, or 御覧, optionally followed by なさい or ください/下さい.
The card spans the connective through the request and retains the verb as lexical
evidence. Tests exercise Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization, both connectives,
spellings, polite forms, standalone ご覧, and ご覧になる controls.

## Limitations

Matching is token-based and cannot always distinguish an instruction from other
uses of ご覧 in context. Adjacent に and の are excluded to avoid common nominal
and honorific constructions; other tokenizations and variants remain unverified.
