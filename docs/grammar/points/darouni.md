# 〜だろうに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Conjecture with regret or contrast.

## Implemented

Matches adjacent Sudachi auxiliary だろう or でしょう and particle に,
including the connective and case-particle analyses of に. The span covers
the auxiliary and particle, leaving the preceding predicate available. Focused
tests cover real Sudachi tokenizations, controls, boundaries, reservation,
card identity and overlap with だろう / でしょう.

## Limitations

The form alone does not prove regret or contrast; context is not classified.
The separate だろう / でしょう card can also match the same occurrence.
Other variants and tokenizations have not been verified.
