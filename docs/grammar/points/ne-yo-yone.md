# ね / よ / よね

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Sentence-final particles: ね often seeks agreement, よ informs or emphasizes,
and よね combines these functions. Context determines their precise force.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent Sudachi final-particle tokens ね and よ are matched after a preceding
  token, including clause-medial discourse uses. Adjacent よ + ね is one match.
  Only the particles are reserved, preserving the preceding lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover the three forms, discourse uses, lexical and part-of-speech
  controls, whitespace, reservation, card examples, and identity registration.
  Standalone initial ね, merged tokenizations and other particle sequences are
  outside this rule's coverage. It does not distinguish pragmatic meanings;
  dictionary expression precedence may suppress a match. Precision and recall
  have not been independently evaluated.
