# 〜ともあろう者が

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Expresses surprise or disappointment at the behavior of someone of a given
standing or role.

## Coverage

Matches noun or pronoun + ともあろう者が / ともあろうものが, leaving the
preceding noun or pronoun available as vocabulary. Sudachi full-dictionary
examples and reservation, identity, spacing and incomplete-form controls are
covered by the rule tests. Other person nouns, particles and analyzer splits
are not yet supported; the matcher cannot verify the speaker's attitude.
