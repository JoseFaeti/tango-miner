# 〜ずともよい

- [Meaning](#meaning)

- [Limitations](#limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Need not do something; literary.

## Verified coverage

Matches an irrealis verb or passive/causative auxiliary followed by auxiliary
ずとも and よい, 良い, いい, よかった or 良かった. The grammar span
leaves the verb available as vocabulary. Tests cover Sudachi's noun and particle
analyses of とも, split と + も, past forms, gaps, negative controls, reservation
and card identity. The shorter 〜ずとも can also produce an overlapping card.

## Limitations

Other inflections and tokenizations have not been assessed. The surface-based
continuation can be ambiguous in unusual contexts; no corpus precision estimate
has been made.
