# 〜ません / 〜ました / 〜ませんでした

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Polite verbal negative, past, and past negative endings.

## Coverage and limitations

Matches the ending after a source-adjacent continuative verb or auxiliary, including
passive and ある, and keeps the preceding word outside the matched span. Sudachi
full dictionary analysis splits 食べませんでした into 食べ / ませ / ん / でし / た;
ました and ません are similarly split. Questions such as 行きませんか also
retain the negative ending. The lexicalized すみません is morphologically
indistinguishable and may also match. Incomplete endings and conditional ましたら
are excluded; other inflected and alternative tokenizations remain unverified.
The polite past copula rule can overlap でした in ませんでした.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, passive, questions, lexical and boundary
controls, reservation, and the registered identity.
