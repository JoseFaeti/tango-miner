# 〜を禁じ得ない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot suppress a feeling.

## Verified coverage

Matches a noun immediately followed by を禁じ得ない or を禁じえない,
including past, continuative, conditional, ず / ぬ and negative polite forms.
The noun remains available as vocabulary. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover
驚き, 涙, 怒り, 悲しみ and 笑い, as well as positive, incomplete and
separated controls. Reservation and card examples are tested.

## Limitations

The noun is not checked semantically, so other objects of 禁じ得ない may also
match. Other spellings and tokenizations have not been assessed. The broader
〜得る / 〜得ない rule can also produce a card for the same occurrence;
the grammar pipeline records overlapping rules independently.
