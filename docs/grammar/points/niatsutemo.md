# 〜にあっても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even in a situation or circumstance.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にあっても, including 有って and 在って
when analyzed as forms of ある. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, meeting (会う), boundaries, reservation,
identity, and overlap with the shorter にあって expression. The general
ある / いる rule can also emit a card for the same occurrence.

Literal existence at a place can also match; the rule cannot reliably distinguish
it from the formal expression. Other spellings and tokenizations remain unverified.
