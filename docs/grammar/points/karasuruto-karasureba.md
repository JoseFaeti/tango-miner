# 〜からすると / 〜からすれば

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior and limitations](#verified-behavior-and-limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

From the standpoint of; judging from.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches a noun or pronoun followed by adjacent Sudachi split-mode C 格助詞
  から and すると or すれば. The card spans the expression and retains the
  preceding noun or pronoun as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover both forms, spacing, unrelated sequences, card identity
  and token reservation. Literal actions starting from a place or person can
  have the same sequence and cannot be distinguished by this matcher.
  Dictionary expression precedence may suppress the card; other tokenizations
  and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unmeasured.
