# 〜であって

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Formal copula in its connective form: being, and is, or a related
connection between clauses.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent copular で + あっ + て after an expression, including
before も, は and ほしい. The preceding expression is retained while the
copula is reserved. Sudachi tests cover connective and contrastive examples,
boundaries and lexical 会って; an integration test covers identity and
reservation.

Sudachi may analyze a locative で followed by あって as the same copula,
so ambiguous readings can match. Other tokenizations and independent
precision and recall remain unverified.
