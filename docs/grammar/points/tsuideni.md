# 〜ついでに

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Taking the opportunity.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent noun ついで and case particle に produce a card meaning “taking the
  opportunity; while at it”, including after verbs, nouns plus の, and standalone
  use. Only ついでに is reserved, retaining attached words as lexical evidence.
  The separate 〜の rule may also reserve の. Focused tests cover Sudachi
  split-mode C, these forms, controls and card evidence.
- Other spellings and tokenizations remain unverified. Matching the pair alone
  does not distinguish every possible contextual meaning.
