# ある / いる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Existence.

## Supported behavior

An adjacent subject, topic, inclusive or location particle (が, は, も, に)
followed by the verb ある / いる produces one shared grammar identity. The
highlight covers the predicate. Supported forms include dictionary, past,
て, ば, polite positive and negative forms, and いる + ない (including past
and ば forms). The corresponding lexical tokens remain available for mining.
Sudachi full-dictionary regressions cover 猫がいる, 木があった, polite forms,
and the unrelated 要る, 入る and auxiliary 〜ている / 〜てある.

## Limitations

The particle is a heuristic, not proof of existential meaning: possession,
events and idiomatic uses may also match, including overlapping grammar points.
Unmarked subjects and intervening words are missed. Standalone ない is not
assigned to this point because its source does not distinguish the missing
ある from other negatives; further forms and tokenizations remain unverified.
Corpus precision and recall have not been measured.
