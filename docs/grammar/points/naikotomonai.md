# 〜ないこともない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Possible, with reservation.

## Coverage

Recognizes adjacent `ないこともない`, `ないこともなかった`,
`ないこともありません` and `ないこともありませんでした` after verb,
adjective, copular and existence predicates. The span starts at the first
ない, leaving the preceding predicate as lexical evidence. The ordinary
verbal negative rule can also report an overlapping match.

Focused Sudachi tests cover these forms, neighboring constructions, boundaries,
reservation and registry identity. A literal statement about something absent
can be indistinguishable from this reading. Contractions and other variants
remain unverified.
