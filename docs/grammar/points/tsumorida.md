# 〜つもりだ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Intention.

## Verified behavior

- A verb in attributive form or verb-negative ない followed by つもり or
  積もり and finite だ/です, including past だった/でした, produces a card.
  The noun and copula are reserved; the attached verb remains available as
  lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, controls and card evidence.
- This pattern can also express a belief or pretense rather than an intention;
  the rule does not distinguish these meanings. Bare and particle-marked
  つもり, negative copulas and other inflections remain outside verified scope.
