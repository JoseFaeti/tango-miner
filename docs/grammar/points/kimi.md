# 〜気味

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Slightly inclined toward a state, often an undesirable one.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent nouns and continuative verb or auxiliary stems followed by
  the Sudachi common noun 気味. Only 気味 is reserved, retaining the host as
  lexical evidence and excluding following predicate material.
- Focused tests cover noun, verb and passive forms, boundaries, standalone
  気味, 不気味, 君 and card evidence. The matcher cannot distinguish every
  noun compound involving the ordinary noun 気味 from the tendency suffix;
  other tokenizations and contexts remain unverified.
