# 〜以来

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Since a time or event.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by separately tokenized 以来 produces a
  grammar card. Only 以来 is reserved; the anchor remains available as lexical
  evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, dates, events,
  demonstratives, boundaries, lexical controls and card evidence.
- Standalone 以来 and kana いらい are left as lexical candidates. The latter
  can also mean 依頼, so kana readings need further disambiguation. Other
  tokenizations and semantic restrictions on nominal anchors remain unverified;
  a nominal adjacency alone does not establish a temporal meaning.
- 〜て以来 is a separate scheduled point and is not matched by this rule.
