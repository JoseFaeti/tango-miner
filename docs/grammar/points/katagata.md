# 〜かたがた

[Contents](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

On the occasion of one activity, also pursue another purpose; formal usage.

## Coverage

Recognizes かたがた and 方々 after an adjacent noun and before an adjacent
non-particle token, reserving only the grammar token. Sudachi full-dictionary
tests cover お礼かたがた伺う, 散歩かたがた買い物, and ご挨拶方々伺う,
alongside plural-person and various-places controls. The noun and following
context are retained as evidence.

This boundary heuristic cannot reliably distinguish every plural-person or
various-places use from the grammar, and can miss other tokenizations or
punctuated examples. Broader source evidence and ambiguity handling remain.
