# 〜ようがない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

No way to.

## Coverage

Matches a verb continuative stem followed by the nominal suffix よう, が or も,
and ない, including past, て and ば forms and adjacent です. The highlighted
span starts at よう, preserving the verb as lexical evidence. Focused Sudachi
tests cover these forms and unrelated よう and 仕様 readings.

Sudachi can tag a verb stem as a noun, as in 調べようがない; these cases are
currently missed to avoid treating arbitrary nouns as verbs. Other inflections,
colloquial forms and alternate tokenizations remain unverified.
