# 〜を背景に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Against the background of.

## Implemented coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by を背景に, including the shared
prefix of を背景にして, を背景にする and を背景にした. The example and
reserved span cover を through に; the preceding noun remains available as a
lexical candidate. Focused tests cover Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization,
controls, reservation and identity.

Literal visual backgrounds also match; the rule does not distinguish them
from contextual or causal backgrounds. Other spellings and tokenizations, and
the related を背景として form, are not verified or implemented.
