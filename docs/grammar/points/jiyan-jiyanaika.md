# 〜じゃん / 〜じゃないか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Recognition, emphasis, or appeal to shared understanding.

## Verified coverage

The rule recognizes Sudachi sentence-final じゃん and じゃないか after
nominal and plain predicate forms, including negative predicates. The matched
example is the suffix alone. Tests cover both forms, lexical じゃんけん,
ordinary negative forms, source examples, and retention of lexical candidates.

## Limitations

じゃないか can also be a literal negative question or occur in an embedded
question, so its surface form alone does not establish the intended meaning.
Other variants, including じゃない without か, are not covered. Tokenization
and contexts beyond the tested forms need further investigation.
