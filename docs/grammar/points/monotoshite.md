# 〜ものとして

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Assuming or treating as.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent attributive verbs, adjectives and auxiliaries followed by
ものとして, including past and な-adjective forms. The predicate and もの
remain available as lexical evidence. The existing 〜として card also matches
and reserves として. Focused Sudachi and integration tests cover forms,
boundaries, card identity and overlap.

Literal things modified by a predicate can share this tokenization and are
also matched; meaning is not disambiguated. Kanji 物 and other tokenizations
are not covered. 〜ものとする is tracked separately.
