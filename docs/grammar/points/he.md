# へ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Direction.

## Verified coverage

Matches a complete Sudachi `へ` case particle (`助詞, 格助詞`) adjacent to a
preceding token, including destinations, recipients, and phrases with an omitted
verb or following の. Only the particle is reserved, preserving neighboring
lexical evidence; dictionary-expression precedence and overlapping grammar
rules still apply.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, lexical and punctuation controls,
reservation, examples and identity. The rule does not distinguish senses.
Standalone and spaced particles, punctuation other than a closing bracket before
the particle, and forms tokenized within a larger token are unsupported. Corpus
precision, recall and effects on lexical candidates have not been measured.
