# 〜に向けて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Toward a goal.

## Verified coverage

The existing [〜に向かって / 〜に向けて rule](nimukatsute-nimukete.md)
matches noun or pronoun + に向けて, including kana spelling, and preserves
the target as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover goals, recipients, physical
aiming, reservation and the separate に向けた form. Physical and figurative
uses are not distinguished; other tokenizations remain unverified.
