# 〜限り

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As long as or to the extent.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent affirmative verbal or i-adjective predicate followed by 限り
  or かぎり produces a card, retaining the preceding words as lexical evidence.
  Auxiliary endings require an adjacent verbal or i-adjective host. Tests cover
  Vる, Vた, Nである, Nであった, 若いかぎり, spacing, reservation and identity.
- Nの限り (including 力の限り and 破壊の限りを尽くす), adjective +
  な限り (including 残念な限りじゃよ), and bare nominal restrictions such
  as 今日限り and 今回かぎり do not produce this card. These words remain
  available to lexical processing; this rule does not reserve them.
- Separately tracked 〜ない限り, 〜限りでは, 〜限りだ and 〜限りの
  are excluded from this rule. Other continuations, contextual distinctions
  remain unverified. Conditional na-adjectives such as 元気な限り are
  currently excluded with extreme-degree な限り uses; distinguishing them
  requires contextual evidence. Separate expression recognition is not added.
- Sudachi's single-token lexical adverb できる限り is retained as a word
  rather than split into a grammar occurrence.
