# 〜までだ

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior and limitations](#verified-behavior-and-limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Nothing more than; all that remains is to do something.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches an adjacent non-past verb or past verb followed by までだ or
  までです. The card spans the particle and copula, retaining the verb.
- Focused Sudachi tests cover both meanings, noun endpoints, spacing,
  unrelated endings, card identity and reservation. Literal endpoints after
  verbs can share these forms. Other inflections and tokenizations, dictionary
  precedence, and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unverified.
