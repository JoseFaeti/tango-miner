# 〜をはじめとして

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Including; starting with a prominent example.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をはじめとして / を始めとして, leaving
surrounding words outside the span. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
controls, reservation and identity. The shorter 〜をはじめ rule also matches
the prefix, producing a separate card. Other tokenizations and semantic
ambiguities remain unverified.
