# 〜せいで

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Because of, undesirable result.

## Verified behavior

- An attributive predicate, noun plus の, or demonstrative followed by
  separately tokenized せい or 所為 and the particle で produces a card.
  Only せいで or 所為で is reserved; the cause remains lexical evidence.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, lexical controls,
  reservation and card evidence.
- Standalone せいで and copular せいでなく are not matched. The rule does
  not determine whether the outcome is undesirable or ironic. Other forms,
  spellings and tokenizations need further validation.
