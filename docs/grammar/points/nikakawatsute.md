# 〜にかかわって

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Involving; relating to or affecting.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にかかわって, に関わって or に拘わって.
The span ends at て, including when followed by いる or きた; the preceding
noun remains lexical evidence. This connective form takes precedence over
[〜に関わる](nikakawaru.md), which retains dictionary and past forms.

Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover these spellings, pronouns, boundaries and
negative controls. Integration tests verify reservation, example surface,
registry identity and precedence against the broader rule. にかかわらず and
にもかかわらず are excluded. Involvement and affecting senses are not
separated. Polite connective forms such as にかかわりまして and other analyzer
tokenizations remain unsupported; no corpus-wide recall assessment was run.
