# 〜というのは

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Topic or explanation.

## Verified coverage

Matches a preceding topic or clause followed by `というのは`, including
`と言うのは` and `っていうのは`. The preceding words remain available as
lexical evidence; the shorter という and って rules yield to this construction.
Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation and card identity.
The same surface can literally mean “the act of saying”, so a match does not
guarantee the explanatory meaning. Contracted forms such as `ってのは` and
other tokenizations are not yet supported.
