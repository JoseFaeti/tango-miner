# 〜かのようだ

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As if.

## Verified behavior

- Matches かのようだ, かのようです, かのようだった and
  かのようでした following an adjacent finite or attributive verb, adjective
  or auxiliary. The predicate remains available as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests use Sudachi full tokenization and cover reservation, card
  examples, ordinary ようだ, 鹿のようだ and the separate かのように and
  かのような constructions.
- Other inflections, spelling variants and tokenizations remain unverified;
  matching does not determine whether the comparison is hypothetical.
