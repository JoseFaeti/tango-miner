# 〜に忍びない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot bear to do something or cannot bring oneself to do it, often because
it would be painful or distressing.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun, pronoun or verb + に忍びない or にしのびない,
including plain past and polite negative forms. The preceding word remains
lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries,
reservation and card identity. Literal inability to endure something can also
match; the rule does not distinguish senses from context. Other inflections and
tokenizations remain unsupported.
