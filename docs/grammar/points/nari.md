# 〜なり

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As soon as; immediately after an action.

## Verified behavior

- A dictionary-form verb followed by Sudachi's conjunctive particle なり
  produces a card and reserves only the particle. Focused tests cover mode C,
  card evidence, reservation and unrelated uses.
- An adjacent VなりVなり choice is excluded, including a comma after the first
  particle. More complex choices, other tokenizations and auxiliary forms are
  unverified. The matcher does not establish that a subsequent action occurs.
