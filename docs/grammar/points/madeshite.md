## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches adjacent Sudachi split-mode C 副助詞 まで, する stem し and
  接続助詞 て after an adjacent token. The card spans までして and preserves
  the preceding words as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover noun, verb-te and demonstrative contexts, unrelated forms,
  spacing, card identity and token reservation. The same sequence can describe
  a literal action up to a place or degree, and the matcher cannot distinguish
  those readings. Dictionary expression precedence may suppress the card; other
  tokenizations and corpus-wide precision and recall remain unmeasured.
