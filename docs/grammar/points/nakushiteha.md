# 〜なくしては

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)
- [Limitations](#limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without, cannot.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by the separate Sudachi tokens
なく (ない adjective), し (する), て and は. The span is なくしては;
the noun remains lexical evidence. Tests cover Sudachi forms, lexical and
boundary controls, reservation, card identity and overlap with 〜なくして.

## Limitations

Other tokenizations and contextual meanings are unverified. The rule does not
require a negative or impossible consequence, so structural matches may be
ambiguous. The shorter 〜なくして card is also collected for the same span.
