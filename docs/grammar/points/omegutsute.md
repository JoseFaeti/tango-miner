# 〜をめぐって

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Concerning or around.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + をめぐって / を巡って, including
をめぐっての; the noun and following の remain outside the grammar span.
Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and identity.
Literal movement around places can share the same form and is not distinguished.
Other spellings and tokenizations are unverified; attributive をめぐる is not
matched.
