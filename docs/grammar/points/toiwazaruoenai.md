# 〜と言わざるを得ない

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Tracked scope](#tracked-scope)
- [Implementation](#implementation)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Have to say; cannot help but conclude.

## Tracked scope

Matches a preceding claim followed directly by と言わざるを得ない or
といわざるをえない, including the negative forms supported by
[〜ざるを得ない](zaruoenai.md). The span starts at と, preserving the claim;
the general 〜ざるを得ない card also matches. Tests cover Sudachi forms,
negative controls, reservation and both card examples. The literal obligation
to say something can have the same form and is not distinguished. Other
segmentations and corpus precision and recall remain unassessed.
