# 〜によって / 〜による

Status: [~]

## Meaning

According to; due to; by means of; because of; via; on the basis of; depending on.
These glosses follow the JMdict entry for によって (1009640); the separate
による entry (1009660) covers overlapping senses.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + によって and による, including によっては
and attributive による. The preceding noun and following words remain lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, reservation and identity.
The separately scheduled hearsay form によると is excluded. Kana よる can
also describe literal reliance or other lexical uses, which remain ambiguous.
Kanji spellings, により, polite and other inflections are not yet covered.
