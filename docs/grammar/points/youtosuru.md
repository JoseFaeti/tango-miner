# 〜ようとする

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Attempt or about to.

## Coverage

An adjacent Sudachi volitional verb followed by と and する matches selected
plain, past, polite, negative, connective and conditional forms of する.
The matched span starts at と because Sudachi combines the volitional ending
with the verb; the verb remains available as lexical evidence. Connective
として can also participate in a separate ている match. Focused analyzer
tests cover godan, ichidan, irregular verbs, imminent events and unrelated
constructions. The form alone cannot distinguish an attempt from imminence.
Other tokenizations and inflections are not yet supported; corpus frequency,
expression precedence and independent precision and recall remain unevaluated.
