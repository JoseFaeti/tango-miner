# 〜きり

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Only or since.

## Verified behavior

- Sudachi split-mode C 副助詞 きり and っきり following an adjacent noun,
  pronoun, or past auxiliary produce a card spanning the particle. This covers
  二人きり, それきり and 出たっきり; the attached word remains lexical evidence.
- Tests cover these forms and lexical uses such as きりがない and 切り取る.
  The matcher does not distinguish the limiting and continuing-state meanings.
  Other tokenizations and attachments remain unverified; dictionary expression
  precedence can suppress a match when a longer expression covers it.
