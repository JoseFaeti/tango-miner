# 〜であれ〜であれ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Whether or.

## Verified coverage

Matches two adjacent であれ endings separated by one or more noun tokens,
with an optional comma before the second alternative. Each ending is recorded
separately so that both alternatives remain available as vocabulary; the
existing 〜であれ / 〜であろうと rule also matches the endings. Focused
tests cover Sudachi forms, controls, reservation and identity.

Longer or non-nominal alternatives, other tokenizations, and independent
precision and recall remain unverified. Individual examples highlight one
ending rather than the whole construction; dictionary expression precedence
may suppress matches.
