# 〜つつある

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An action or change in progress, often a gradual development.

## Implemented coverage

Matches an adjacent verb or verbal auxiliary in the continuative form followed
by つつ and ある, including plain, past, polite positive, て and conditional
forms of ある. The card reserves つつ and its following predicate while keeping
the preceding verb available as a lexical candidate. Sudachi full-dictionary
tests cover 変化しつつある, 増えつつあります and 失われつつあった, as well
as unrelated 筒 and the separate つつ and つつも constructions.

The matcher identifies morphology, not whether the context describes gradual
change. Other inflections, spellings and tokenizations remain unverified.
