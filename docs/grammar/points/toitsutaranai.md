# 〜といったらない

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Indescribably; extremely.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent といったらない, と言ったらない and colloquial ったらない,
including the tested past form なかった. The preceding expression remains
lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi boundaries, controls, reservation
and identity. Literal or otherwise ambiguous uses may also match; the rule does
not distinguish meaning. Polite forms such as といったらありません and other
inflections remain unsupported.
