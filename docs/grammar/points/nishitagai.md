# 〜にしたがい

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As a change progresses; in accordance with. Formal connective counterpart of
〜にしたがって.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by に従い
or にしたがい, including before a comma. Only に and the connective verb are
reserved; the preceding word remains available as vocabulary evidence.
Sudachi-based tests cover both spellings, noun and verb attachments, punctuation
boundaries, longer inflections and lexical controls. Integration tests cover
reservation, source examples, registry identity and legacy identity.

The rule also accepts literal following, such as 彼に従い歩く; it does not
separate that reading from accordance or correlated change. Sentence-final and
particle-followed uses are excluded. Other tokenizations and corpus precision
remain unverified, so coverage is partial.
