# 〜てしかたがない / 〜てしょうがない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot help feeling.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent て / で plus 仕方, しかた, しょう, しよう or 仕様
and ない, with optional が, including past, conditional, なくて and polite
ありません forms. Sudachi's single-token しかたない is also supported.
The preceding verb, adjective or adjectival noun remains lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover observed tokenization, spelling, boundaries, reservation,
card examples and identity.

The construction can also express an unavoidable situation rather than an
intense feeling; morphology alone cannot distinguish these readings. Standalone
仕方がない is outside this point. Other spellings, inflections and unusual
tokenizations remain unverified.
