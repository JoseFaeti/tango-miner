Status: [~]

## Meaning

Toward a person or object; in contrast to, or whereas (formal).

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に対し, including contrast with nominalized
clauses such as 兄は静かなのに対し、弟は賑やかだ. Only に対し is
reserved; the preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Sudachi full dictionary
regressions cover direction, contrast, pronouns, punctuation, sentence-final
fragments, and whitespace boundaries. Integration tests verify reservation,
card examples, registry identity, and legacy identity.

## Limitations

Adjacent particles and auxiliaries are excluded so に対して, に対しまして,
and inflected lexical verbs do not become shortened cards. に対する is also
outside this rule. Literal and grammatical uses of bare に対し are not
semantically distinguished. Other tokenizations remain unverified. No corpus
pipeline run was performed; this point remains partial.
