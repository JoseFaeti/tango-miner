# 〜に則って

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In accordance with a rule or standard.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に則って, including kana spelling. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and identity. Attributive に則った
and other inflections or tokenizations are not covered. Kana のっとって can
be ambiguous with 乗っ取って; analyzer lemma selection determines the result.
