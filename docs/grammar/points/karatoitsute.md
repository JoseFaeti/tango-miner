# 〜からといって

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Just because one condition holds does not mean that a conclusion follows.

## Coverage

- Matches adjacent からといって and からと言って after a verb, adjective,
  or auxiliary when Sudachi identifies から as a connective particle.
- Tests cover the card identity, reservation, overlap with causal から, and
  quoted source and temporal から controls. The preceding clause remains lexical.
- The matcher does not verify the following conclusion; literal quotations
  with a connective から can be ambiguous. Colloquial からって and other
  variants are not yet covered.
