# 〜ともあれ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Be that as it may.

## Verified coverage

Matches the Sudachi adverb ともあれ, including standalone use and expressions
such as 何はともあれ and 理由はともあれ. The preceding words remain available
as lexical evidence; other grammar rules may reserve their own tokens. An
adjacent ば is excluded because Sudachi can analyze ともあれば as this adverb
followed by ば. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, controls, reservation and
identity. Other spellings and tokenizations are not covered; dictionary
expression precedence can suppress a match when enabled.
