## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に代えて, preserving the preceding noun
as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation
and identity. Literal uses such as 命に代えても are included; overlapping
dictionary expressions can suppress grammar occurrences. Other tokenizations,
polite forms and semantic distinctions remain unverified. に替えて and
に代わって are separately tracked.
