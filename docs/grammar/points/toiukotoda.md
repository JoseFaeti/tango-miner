# 〜ということだ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Meaning or hearsay.

## Verified coverage

Matches finite predicates followed by `ということだ`, including `と言う`,
`っていう`, `事`, polite `です`, and past `だった` / `でした`. The span
reserves the construction while retaining the preceding predicate; the shorter
attributive という rule yields to it. Dictionary-expression precedence applies.
Focused tests cover analyzer tokenization, forms, controls, reservation and card
identity. Meaning and hearsay are not distinguished, and literal interpretations
can be ambiguous. Nominal predicates without a copula, other inflections and
contractions such as ってことだ remain unsupported.
