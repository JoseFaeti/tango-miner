# 〜くせに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Despite, with criticism.

## Verified behavior

- Split-mode C くせに and クセに after an adjacent attributive verb,
  adjective or auxiliary, or a noun or pronoun followed by の, produce one
  card. Only くせに is reserved; the preceding words remain available.
  Sentence-final and negative forms are included. Dictionary expression
  precedence can suppress a match.
- An immediately following form of なる prevents reservation, preserving
  habit expressions such as 食べるクセになりそう. This also misses a
  grammatical concession immediately followed by なる.
- Focused tests cover these forms, lexical 癖, reservation and card examples.
  Other kana habit uses such as 悪いくせに悩む share the same analysis and may
  be matched; semantics are not distinguished. Kanji 癖に and other
  tokenizations are not covered, including potentially grammatical uses.
