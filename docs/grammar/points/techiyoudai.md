# 〜てちょうだい

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An informal request to do something; ないでちょうだい asks someone not to do it.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent verbal て/で followed by ちょうだい or 頂戴, and verbal
ないでちょうだい. The span retains the preceding verb as lexical evidence.
Focused Sudachi tests cover both connectives, spellings, negative requests,
standalone nouns, separated tokens, and 頂戴する. Reservation and card identity
are also tested.

## Limitations

Other spellings and tokenizations are unverified. Context can still make a
matched expression ambiguous; adjacent 頂戴する is excluded as a receiving verb.
