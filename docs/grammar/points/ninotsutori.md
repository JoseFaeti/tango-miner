# 〜に則り

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In accordance with a rule or standard, in a formal connective form.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に則り, including kana spelling, while
retaining the preceding noun as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi
tokenization, boundaries, polite and lexical controls, reservation and identity.
An immediately attached auxiliary is excluded. Kana のっとり may be ambiguous
with 乗っ取り; analyzer lemma selection determines the result. Other
tokenizations and inflections are not covered; に則って has a separate rule.
