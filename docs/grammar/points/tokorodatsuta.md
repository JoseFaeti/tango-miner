# 〜ところだった

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Nearly happened.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent attributive verb followed by ところ or 所 and だった or でした
  produces a card. The marker and copula are reserved while the verb remains
  available as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, controls,
  card identity and the overlap with 〜ところだ.
- The form can also describe a past intention, situation or literal place;
  the rule cannot distinguish these readings from a near event. ている forms
  remain with 〜ところだ. Negative verbs and other variants are not covered.
