# 〜わりに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Considering; for; although: the outcome differs from what the preceding
standard might lead one to expect.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent attributive verbs, adjectives and auxiliaries, or nouns and
pronouns followed by の, before わりに, 割に or 割りに. Focused Sudachi tests
cover negative and past predicates, な-adjectives, noun attachment, spelling,
boundaries and lexical controls; card identity and token reservation are also
tested. The preceding words remain lexical evidence.

The attachment alone cannot distinguish the contrastive meaning from literal
uses of 割 or 割り, and semantic context is not checked. Standalone and other
attachments remain unverified.
