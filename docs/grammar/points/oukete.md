# 〜を受けて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In response to or following an event, report, or other development.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + を受けて or をうけて, retaining the
preceding noun as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal uses such as receiving a ball
are structurally indistinguishable and may also match; the rule does not
resolve meaning from context. Other inflections and tokenizations are not
covered.
