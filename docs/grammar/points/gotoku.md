## Meaning

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + のごとく / の如く, preserving the
  comparison target as lexical evidence. The related がごとく and かのごとく
  constructions have separate rules.
- Sudachi full tests cover both spellings, reservation and card examples,
  with controls for related constructions, ごとき, spacing and 五徳.
- Other attachments, tokenizations and forms remain unverified; contextual
  meaning is not resolved.
