# 〜のか / 〜んか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Question or realization.

## Tracked scope

Matches adjacent terminal or attributive verbs, adjectives, or auxiliaries followed
by the nominalizing particle `の` or `ん` and question particle `か`. This covers
direct and embedded questions, including `なのか` and `のかどうか`; the span is
`のか` or `んか`; those tokens are reserved when the card is active. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover
both markers, predicate forms, embedded questions and lexical controls.

Nominalized literal questions can share this form and remain review candidates.
Merged tokenizations and corpus precision and recall have not been evaluated.
