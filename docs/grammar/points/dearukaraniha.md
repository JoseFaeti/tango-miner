# 〜であるからには

[Contents](#meaning) · [Verified behavior and limitations](#verified-behavior-and-limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Given that something is the case, a consequence or commitment follows.

## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches adjacent copular であるからには, retaining the preceding predicate.
  The overlapping 〜である, 〜からには and 〜から / 〜ので cards can also
  be emitted; dictionary expression precedence can suppress a match.
- Focused Sudachi split-mode C tests cover noun and adjectival predicates,
  spacing, unrelated and incomplete forms, card identity and reservation.
  The matcher does not verify the consequence and inherits 〜である's copular
  ambiguity. Other tokenizations and corpus-wide precision and recall remain
  unmeasured.
