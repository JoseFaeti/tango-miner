# 〜ことになっている

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An established rule or arrangement. Matches a finite verb or verbal negative
followed by split ことになっている, including selected past, polite, negative
and contracted ている forms. The predicate stays available as lexical evidence.
Noun predicates, merged analyzer tokens and other forms are not covered; the
construction can also describe a supposed state, so context affects its meaning.
Corpus precision and recall have not been evaluated.
