# 〜どころか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Far from; let alone; on the contrary, often introducing a stronger or contrasting reality.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent どころか after an attached expression, including nouns, verbs,
and adjectives, with Sudachi's two adverbial-particle tokens. The preceding
expression remains available as lexical evidence; どころ and か are reserved
for grammar. Focused tests cover tokenization, boundaries, lexical controls,
reservation and identity.

The matcher does not establish the meaning of the surrounding clauses. It does
not cover punctuation-separated or sentence-initial uses, other spellings or
tokenizations. Dictionary expression precedence can suppress a match when enabled.
