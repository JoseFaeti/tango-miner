# 〜を通じて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Through or over a period.

## Verified coverage

The shared 〜を通して / 〜を通じて rule matches adjacent noun or pronoun
+ を通じて and polite を通じまして for means, intermediaries, and duration.
It keeps the preceding noun as lexical evidence. Analyzer-based tests cover
these forms, boundaries, and controls; reservation and card identity are also
tested. The shared card identity is retained. See [the combined point](otooshite-otsuujite.md)
for its coverage and limitations. Other spellings and tokenizations remain
unverified; context is not used to disambiguate literal uses.
