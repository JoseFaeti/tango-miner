# 〜といったら / 〜といえば

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Speaking of or exclamation.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent といったら and といえば, including 言ったら and 言えば,
after a noun, pronoun, adjective or verb. The preceding topic remains lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, reservation and identity.
The separate といったらない rule handles the overlapping extreme-degree form.
Literal conditional quotations can also match; the rule does not distinguish
their meaning. Colloquial variants and other tokenizations remain unverified.
