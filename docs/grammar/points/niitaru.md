# 〜に至る

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Come to a result or reach a point or stage. Literal spatial endpoints can also
match because the distinction is semantic.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent noun, pronoun, verb, or auxiliary + に + 至る, 至った, or
至ります, including kana spellings when analyzed as the verb 至る. The
grammar span starts at に; the preceding expression remains available.
Focused token and reservation tests cover these forms and lexical controls.
Separate rules handle に至って and に至るまで. Other inflections and analyzer
variants remain unverified; kana can be analyzed as another verb.
