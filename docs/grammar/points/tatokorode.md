# 〜たところで

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent verb, past auxiliary た/だ, ところ/所 and particle で
  produce a card. Only ところ/所 and で are reserved, preserving the verb
  and past auxiliary. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, controls, identity,
  reservation and overlap with 〜ところで and 〜たところ.
- Literal places such as 昨日行った所で also match: the rule cannot confirm
  a concessive meaning. Negative and polite past forms remain unverified.
