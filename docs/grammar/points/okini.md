# 〜を機に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Taking an event as an occasion or opportunity for a change.

## Implemented coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by を機に, including the shared prefix
of を機にして, を機にする and を機にした. The example and reserved span
cover を through に; the preceding noun remains a lexical candidate. Focused
tests cover Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization, controls, reservation and
identity. The matcher does not distinguish contextual readings, including
literal uses of 機.

Other spellings and tokenizations, and the related を機として form, have
not been implemented or verified; the point remains partial.
