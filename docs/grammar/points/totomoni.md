# 〜とともに

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Together with; as a change proceeds; or at the same time as another property
or event.

## Verified coverage

Matches a noun, pronoun, verb, adjective or auxiliary followed by adjacent
とともに or と共に, when Sudachi splits the expression into と, とも/共,
and に. The preceding token remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover
both spellings, concurrent change and properties, boundaries, reservation and
card identity. A sequence such as 彼とともに会う can also have an analysis
involving a friend; the rule does not disambiguate meaning. Other tokenizations
and variants remain unverified.
