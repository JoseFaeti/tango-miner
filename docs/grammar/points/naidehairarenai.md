# 〜ないではいられない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot help doing.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verbal negative followed by で(は)いられない, including
居られない, past, negative polite, continuative and conditional forms with
separate Sudachi tokens. The preceding verb remains vocabulary; the grammar
span starts at ない. Tests cover godan, ichidan, する and 来る verbs,
positive and incomplete endings, gaps, an adjective control, reservation and
card examples. Sudachi can analyze で as an auxiliary before は.

## Limitations

The construction can also describe literal inability to remain without doing
something; token matching cannot distinguish that reading. Other segmentations
and inflections have not been assessed.
