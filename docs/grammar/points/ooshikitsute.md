# 〜を押し切って

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Overriding objections or resistance; proceeding despite opposition.

## Coverage

Matches contiguous noun + を + 押し切って / おしきって with nouns
反対, 抵抗, 批判, 反発, 異論, 異議 or 制止. Only を through て is
reserved; the noun remains available as vocabulary evidence.
Sudachi full mode C verifies 押し切っ / 押し切る and おしきっ / おしきる
followed by particle て, as in 両親の反対を押し切って結婚した。

`src/grammar_rules/rules/ooshikitsute/test_index.py` covers these forms,
literal cutting and competitive controls, other inflections, adjacency,
reservation, example surface and grammar identity. Examples are constructed
regressions; corpus precision has not been measured.

## Limitations

Other objects remain lexical candidates for review. Broader object coverage
and mixed spellings remain unverified. Plain, past and negative forms are
outside this connective scope.
