## Coverage

The existing [〜に代わって / 〜の代わりに](nikawatsute-nokawarini.md)
card covers adjacent noun or pronoun + に代わって / にかわって. It retains
the preceding noun as lexical evidence and reserves the matched grammar tokens.
Focused analyzer and integration tests cover both spellings, representation,
boundaries, card identity and reservation. The form can also express literal
replacement; kana spellings can be ambiguous. Other attachments and variants
remain unverified.
