## Meaning

Toward.

## Verified coverage

The existing [〜に向かって / 〜に向けて rule](nimukatsute-nimukete.md)
matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に向かって, including kana spelling,
and preserves the target as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover physical
direction, goals, recipients, boundaries, reservation and identity. Physical
and figurative uses are not distinguished; other tokenizations remain unverified.
