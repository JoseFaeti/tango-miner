## Meaning

Not only; as well as.

## Verified coverage

lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, neighboring forms,
boundaries, reservation and card identity. The rule does not require a following
も or distinguish contextual senses; sentence-initial usage and other analyzer
tokenizations remain unsupported.
