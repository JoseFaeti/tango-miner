## Verified coverage

Matches a noun or pronoun followed by adjacent に先立って, including
に先立っての. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover
Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity. Literal uses
such as 夫に先立って死ぬ share the same pattern and are also matched; the
rule does not distinguish their meaning. Other inflections and tokenizations
are not supported.
