## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にとって and the polite にとりまして,
including にとっての and にとっては; the preceding word remains lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and identity.

can also mean taking, so the matcher cannot disambiguate all literal uses.
