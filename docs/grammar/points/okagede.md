# 〜おかげで

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Thanks to; owing to a cause, often with a favorable result.

## Verified behavior

- An attributive predicate, noun plus の, or demonstrative followed by
  separately tokenized おかげ or お陰 and the particle で produces a card.
  Only the expression is reserved; the cause remains lexical evidence.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, lexical controls,
  reservation and card evidence.
- Standalone おかげで and copular おかげでなく are not matched. The rule
  does not determine whether the outcome is favorable or sarcastic. Other
  spellings and tokenizations, including お蔭, need further validation.
