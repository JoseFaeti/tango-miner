# 〜てたまらない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An overwhelming feeling or urge; unbearably, cannot help feeling.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent て / で followed by たまらない or 堪らない, including
plain past, なくて, conditional and polite negative forms. The preceding
verb, adjective or adjectival noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests
cover Sudachi tokenization, spelling, boundaries, reservation and identity.

Kana たまる can also mean accumulating: the matcher cannot determine the
intended sense from morphology alone. 貯まる is excluded as a lexical control.
Other spellings, literary negatives, and unusual tokenizations remain unverified.
