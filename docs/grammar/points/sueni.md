# 〜末に

Status: [~]

## Meaning

After a process, often involving effort or deliberation, a result is reached.

## Implemented coverage

Matches past-tense verbs and noun + の followed by 末に or すえに, with
adjacent Sudachi tokens. The card reserves only 末に or すえに, retaining
the preceding process as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover both spellings,
voiced past tense, nominal attachment, boundaries, card evidence and 週末.

The surface pattern cannot distinguish a literal end, such as 道の末に,
from a process result; such matches remain ambiguous. 末の and other forms
without に are not covered. Further source evidence is needed to assess these
limitations and other tokenizations.
