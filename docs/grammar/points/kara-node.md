# 〜から / 〜ので

Status: [~]

## Meaning and tracked scope

Reason or cause.

## Verified behavior

- A predicate in terminal form followed by the conjunctive particle kara, or
  an attributive predicate followed by nominalizing no and copular de, produces
  a shared card. Noun and na-adjective copulas, polite and past forms, and
  sentence-final kara are supported. Only the marker is reserved; the predicate
  remains lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover supported forms, case-particle spatial and temporal kara,
  reservation, and examples. Nominalized no followed by copular de can be
  ambiguous and may match without expressing a reason. Analyzer mis-tokenizations
  and contractions remain unsupported.
