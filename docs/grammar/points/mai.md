# 〜まい

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Negative volition or conjecture.

## Tracked scope

Recognizes the auxiliary `まい` after adjacent verbal dictionary forms,
ichidan and 来る stems, and supported verbal auxiliaries including `ます`.
The card highlights `まい` and retains the verb as lexical evidence. Tests
cover Sudachi forms, lexical controls, boundaries and card creation. Both
`〜まい` and the more specific `〜ではあるまいか` can be collected for the
same sentence. Sudachi can tokenize `すまい` and `しまい` as nouns, so these
ambiguous negative-volition forms are not recognized in that analysis. Other
segmentations and corpus precision and recall remain unassessed.
