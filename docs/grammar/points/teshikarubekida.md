# 〜てしかるべきだ

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Naturally should; a consequence is appropriate or deserved.

## Verified coverage

Matches verb or auxiliary continuative + て / で + しかる / 然る + べき,
including attributive uses and present or past plain and polite copulas. The
preceding verb remains lexical evidence. Tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
reservation, card identity, and unrelated 叱る and noun + で. The existing
べき rule can also produce an overlapping card.

Other inflections, negative forms, and alternative tokenizations remain
unverified. Context may be needed to assess whether the consequence is deserved.
