## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches a noun or pronoun followed by adjacent Sudachi split-mode C 格助詞
  から and 言うと or 言えば, including hiragana spellings. The expression is
  reserved while the preceding noun or pronoun remains lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover both forms, spacing, unrelated sequences, card identity
  and reservation. Literal speech by a person can share the same form and is
  indistinguishable to this matcher. Dictionary expression precedence may suppress
  the card; other tokenizations and corpus-wide precision and recall are unmeasured.
