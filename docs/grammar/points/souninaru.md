# 〜そうになる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Nearly happens.

## Supported forms

An adjacent verb continuative stem followed by そうに and なる is recognized
in plain, past, polite, negative, and て forms, for example 転びそうになった
and 倒れそうになっている. The grammar span starts at そう, leaving the
verb available as vocabulary. The て form can overlap with 〜ている.

Focused tests use Sudachi full-dictionary tokenization and check reservation,
examples, inflections, spacing, and adjective and hearsay controls. Recognition
depends on the analyzer's verb-stem and そう segmentation; other inflections and
alternative tokenizations are not yet covered. Some matched contexts can also
express becoming likely rather than an event that almost occurred.
