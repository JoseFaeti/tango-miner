## Meaning

Non-exhaustive enumeration, sometimes expressing uncertainty or mixed feelings.

## Verified coverage

Matches repeated Sudachi `やら` particles after adjacent nouns, pronouns,
numerals, verbs, adjectives, adjectival nouns or auxiliaries. Each marker is
reserved separately, preserving the listed words. Focused Sudachi tests cover
noun, adjective, verb and past forms, three items, a single `何やら`, verb
`やらない`, sentence boundaries and spacing. Grammar identity and reservation
are also tested.

The matcher pairs markers within a sentence without determining whether they
belong to the same enumeration; unrelated uses can therefore be paired. It
does not cover single markers, alternate tokenizations or spaced attachment.
Corpus precision, recall and dictionary-expression interactions are unmeasured.
