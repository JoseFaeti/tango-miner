# 〜がち

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Tending to do something or be in a particular state, often undesirable.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent verb continuative stems and nouns followed by the Sudachi
  形状詞的 suffix がち. The suffix alone is reserved, preserving the host as
  lexical evidence; following な, です and other predicate material is excluded.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenizations, boundaries, lexical controls and
  card identity. The matcher does not assess whether a tendency is undesirable;
  other tokenizations and contexts remain unverified.
