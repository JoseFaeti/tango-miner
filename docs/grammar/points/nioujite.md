# 〜に応じて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Depending on; in accordance with.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に応じて, including kana spelling and
polite に応じまして. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal
responses such as 声に応じて also match: the rule cannot distinguish these
from the depending-on meaning. Other forms such as に応じた and other
tokenizations remain unverified.
