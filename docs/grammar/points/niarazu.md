# 〜にあらず

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Is not; literary.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にあらず, に有らず, に非ず, にあらざる and
にあらざれば following an expression. The preceding expression remains lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi splits, boundaries, reservation and card
identity. にあらずして is captured through ず, leaving して outside the span.

The same form can express literal nonexistence at a location, as in ここにあらず;
the matcher does not distinguish that reading. Other spellings, tokenizations and
inflections, including the full ずんば construction, remain unverified.
