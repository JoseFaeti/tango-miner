# 〜に向かって / 〜に向けて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Toward or aimed at.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に向かって / に向けて, including kana
spellings. The target remains lexical evidence. Literal movement and physical
aiming also match; the rule does not distinguish these senses from figurative
goals. Other inflections and tokenizations remain unverified. Focused tests
cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation, and identity.
