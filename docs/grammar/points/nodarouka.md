# 〜のだろうか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Wondering or asking why or whether something might be so.

## Tracked scope

Matches adjacent terminal or attributive verbs, adjectives or auxiliaries followed
by nominalizing `の` or `ん`, `だろう` or `でしょう`, and question particle
`か`. The reserved span runs from the marker through `か`. Direct and embedded
questions are included. Tests cover colloquial and polite forms, predicate types,
lexical controls, card identity and reservation.

Possessive noun + `のだろうか` is intentionally excluded because it can ask
whose item something is. Merged tokenizations and corpus precision and recall
remain unverified. Other overlapping grammar rules may also produce cards.
