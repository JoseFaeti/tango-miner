# 〜には当たらない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Does not warrant doing something; does not amount to or constitute something.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent には当たらない and にはあたらない after a noun, pronoun or
verb, including tested past, polite negative, continuative and conditional forms.
The preceding expression remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover source
spans, controls, reservation and identity.

The rule does not distinguish the grammatical sense from literal uses of 当たる,
such as not hitting a wall. Other tokenizations and inflections remain unverified.
