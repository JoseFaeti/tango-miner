# 〜て以来

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Ever since doing.

## Verified behavior

- [Rule](../../../src/grammar_rules/rules/te_irai/index.py) and
  [tests](../../../src/grammar_rules/rules/te_irai/test_index.py): an adjacent
  continuative verb or auxiliary, connective て or で, and nominal 以来
  produce the `te_irai` card. Only て以来 or で以来 is reserved, leaving
  the preceding verb as lexical evidence. Tests cover Sudachi split-mode C,
  boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and the separate nominal 〜以来 card.
- Kana いらい, merged or differently tokenized forms are not covered; semantic
  continuity and wider corpus precision and recall remain unverified.
