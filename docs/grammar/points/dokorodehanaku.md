# 〜どころではなく

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Far from; rather than. The continuative form can contrast an expectation
with a more extreme reality, or describe an inability to do something.

## Verified coverage

Matches attached どころではなく, どころじゃなく and どころでなく after
a preceding expression, retaining that expression as lexical evidence.
Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation, identity,
and overlap with 〜どころではない and 〜ではなく. Those rules can produce
additional cards for the same occurrence.

The matcher does not distinguish contrast from inability by context. It excludes
なくて and なくなる; other forms and tokenizations remain unverified. Dictionary
expression precedence can suppress matches when enabled.
