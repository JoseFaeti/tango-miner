## Meaning

Throughout; over a period of ...; over a span of ... (JMdict entry 2013910).

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にわたって, including 渡って. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal crossing uses with に are
structurally ambiguous and also match. Other forms, including にわたる,
にわたり and past or polite inflections, remain unsupported. Dictionary-defined
にわたって is recovered into this identity when its complete gloss set matches,
including occurrences missed by the structural matcher.
