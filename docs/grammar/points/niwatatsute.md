# 〜にわたって

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Over a range or period.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にわたって, including 渡って. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal crossing uses with に are
structurally ambiguous and also match. Other forms, including にわたる,
にわたり and past or polite inflections, remain unsupported.
