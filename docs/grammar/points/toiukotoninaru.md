# 〜ということになる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Result or implication.

## Verified coverage

Matches finite verbal, adjectival and copular predicates followed by
`ということになる`, including `と言う`, `っていう`, `事`, past `なった`
and polite `なります` / `なりました`. The span reserves the construction
and retains the preceding predicate; shorter overlapping grammar yields to it.
Focused tests cover analyzer tokenization, forms, controls, reservation and
card identity. Literal interpretations can be ambiguous. Nominal predicates
without a copula, negative and continuative endings, and contractions such as
`ってことになる` remain unsupported.
