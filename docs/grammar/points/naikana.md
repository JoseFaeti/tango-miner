# 〜ないかな

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Negative wondering can express a wish or an indirect request: 雨が降らないかな
(I hope it rains), 手伝ってくれないかな (I wonder if you could help).
It can also be a literal negative question; context is needed to distinguish these.

## Coverage

Matches the complete negative ない followed by the final particles かな or
かなあ, including verbal and adjectival ない. The card highlights ないかな;
the existing 〜かな / 〜かしら rule may also match the ending. The matcher
does not distinguish wishes and requests from literal negative wondering.
Other variants and broader source evidence remain to be investigated.

Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, negative and lexical controls,
adjacency, card identity, and token reservation.
