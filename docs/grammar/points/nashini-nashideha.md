# 〜なしに / 〜なしでは

Status: [~]

## Meaning and coverage

Without something; なしでは marks a condition where something is absent.
The rule recognizes adjacent noun or pronoun + なしに / なしでは, including
無し, and continuative verb forms such as 助け as nominalized antecedents.
It reserves only the construction, retaining the antecedent. Sudachi full
dictionary tests cover these forms, spacing, and the lexical noun 梨.

## Limitations

The matcher does not interpret the following clause, so cannot establish the
conditional meaning or distinguish every lexical ambiguity. Antecedent and
tokenization variants, including colloquial なしじゃ, need further evidence.
