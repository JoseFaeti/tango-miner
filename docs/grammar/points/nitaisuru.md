# 〜に対する

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Toward or regarding a person, thing or action; attributive form.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent noun, pronoun or nominalizing の + に対する / にたいする
followed by a noun. Only the particle and verb are reserved; surrounding words
remain lexical evidence. Sudachi full dictionary tests verify both spellings,
nominal attachments, boundaries, inflected and connective controls, reservation,
example surfaces and stable card identity.

The structural heuristic also matches literal opposition before a noun; it does
not distinguish that reading semantically. Sentence-final uses and past, negative
or polite inflections are outside verified coverage. Connective forms have
separate rules for [〜に対し](nitaishi.md) and [〜に対して](nitaishite.md).
