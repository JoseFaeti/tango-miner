## Meaning

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by に比する, に比して,
に比し, or に比した when Sudachi identifies 比する as a verb. The comparison
target remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries,
reservation, and identity. Literal comparisons can also match. Polite, passive,
kana, other inflections and other analyzer tokenizations remain unsupported or
unverified.
