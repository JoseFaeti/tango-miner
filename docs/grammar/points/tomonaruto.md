# 〜ともなると

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

When it comes to a particular situation or once a certain stage is reached.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent ともなると after a preceding expression, including nouns and
verb clauses. The preceding expression remains available as lexical evidence.
Tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity.

The sequence can also be literal, such as 二人ともなると, and is not
disambiguated. Related ともなれば and other tokenizations are not covered.
