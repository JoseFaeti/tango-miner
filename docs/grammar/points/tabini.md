# 〜たびに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Whenever.

## Verified behavior

- An attributive verb or noun plus の followed by たびに or 度に produces a
  card meaning “whenever; every time”. Only たびに / 度に is reserved, retaining
  the attached word as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C,
  both spellings, nominal and verb forms, lexical controls and card evidence.
- Other tokenizations, omitted に and more complex predicates remain unverified.
