# 〜ずにはおかない

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)
- [Limitations](#limitations)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Inevitably causes something to happen, or is determined not to leave an
action undone.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb in the irrealis form followed by ずにはおかない or
ずには置かない, including past, negative polite, continuative and conditional
forms with separate Sudachi tokens. The preceding verb remains vocabulary;
the grammar span starts at ず. Tests cover causative verbs, incomplete and
positive endings, gaps, lexical controls, reservation and overlap with 〜ずに.

## Limitations

The same sequence can have a literal reading that token matching cannot
distinguish. Other segmentations and inflections have not been assessed.
