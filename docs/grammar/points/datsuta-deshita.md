# だった / でした

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Past copula.

## Verified behavior

- Matches adjacent auxiliary sequences だっ + た and でし + た after a
  source-adjacent predicate, reserving the suffix and retaining preceding words.
- Focused tests cover nominal and adjectival predicates, present, conditional,
  listing, lexical, whitespace and part-of-speech controls, reservation, card
  identity and overlap with a longer grammar point.
- Merged tokenizations are not recovered. This surface-based rule can overlap
  longer constructions, including negative polite predicates; precedence with
  dictionary expressions depends on the pipeline. Precision and recall have
  not been independently evaluated.
