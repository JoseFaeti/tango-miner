# 〜にすぎない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Merely.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にすぎない and に過ぎない after a preceding expression,
including plain past, なくて, conditional and polite negative forms. The
preceding expression remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi
forms, boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Literal readings of 過ぎる can share these tokens and cannot be distinguished
reliably by this matcher. Other inflections and tokenizations remain unverified.
