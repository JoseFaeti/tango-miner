# 〜ないまでも

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if a higher degree or condition is not reached, a lesser one holds.

## Implemented

The rule matches a separate negative ない auxiliary or adjective immediately
followed by the particles まで and も. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover
verbal, adjectival, copular and existential examples, lexical lookalikes,
token boundaries, reservation and card identity. The preceding predicate is
retained as component evidence; the general negative card may also match.

## Limitations

The token sequence alone cannot establish the intended concessive meaning.
Other tokenizations and corpus-level precision and recall remain unassessed.
