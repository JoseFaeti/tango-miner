# 〜に及ばない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

No need to do something; does not reach or equal something.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent に及ばない and におよばない after a noun, pronoun or verb,
including tested past, polite negative, continuative and conditional forms.
Sudachi sometimes analyzes に as a copula, which is accepted here. The preceding
expression remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover both meanings, source
spans, controls, reservation and identity.

The rule does not distinguish the idiomatic and literal senses of 及ぶ; a literal
failure to reach a place or extent can also match. Other tokenizations and
inflections remain unverified. The separate には及ばない rule handles the form with は.
