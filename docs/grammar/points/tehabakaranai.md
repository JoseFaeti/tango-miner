# 〜てはばからない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Openly without hesitation.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb followed by て or で and はばからない or 憚らない,
including past, negative polite, continuative and conditional forms in Sudachi
full-dictionary tokenization. The preceding verb remains vocabulary; the grammar
span begins at て or で. Tests cover spelling variants, lexical and incomplete
controls, gaps, reservation and card examples.

## Limitations

The same sequence can express a literal lack of hesitation; matching does not
distinguish contextual readings. Other tokenizations and inflections remain
unassessed.
