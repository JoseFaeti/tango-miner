## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にしてみると, preserving the preceding
word as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries,
reservation and identity. Kana にしてみると can also mean trying to make
something a certain way; this literal use is ambiguous and can be matched.
Kanji 見ると and the separately tracked にしてみれば are excluded. Other
tokenizations and attachments remain unverified.
