# 〜ことだ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Advice or exclamation.

## Verified behavior

- Attributive nonpast verbs, verb-negative ない, i-adjectives, and na-adjectives
  followed by こと/事 and だ/です produce a card. The expression tokens are
  reserved while the attached predicate remains available as lexical evidence.
- The longer ということだ construction is left to its own rule. Focused tests
  cover Sudachi forms, lexical controls, card evidence, and this overlap.
- Context is needed to distinguish advice or exclamation from ordinary
  nominalized statements, so matches can be ambiguous. Past, negative copulas,
  and other variants are outside the verified scope.
