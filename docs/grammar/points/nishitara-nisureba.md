# 〜にしたら / 〜にすれば

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)
- [Limitations and related forms](#limitations-and-related-forms)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

From someone's perspective.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にしたら / にすれば after pronouns, person names, and a
conservative lexical set of human roles and viewpoint-bearing groups (including
子供, 先生, 政府 and 会社). The preceding subject remains lexical evidence.
An earlier を in the same local clause rejects an object-to-result reading,
including 彼をリーダーにしたら and 彼を先生にすれば.

Focused Sudachi regressions reject ローストチキンにしたら, 記事にしたら,
前にすれば, 目の前にしたら and お茶にしたら. They retain pronoun, name,
human-role, group and 私たち examples, and verify boundaries, reservation and
identity.

## Limitations and related forms

This is a conservative subject heuristic, not semantic parsing. Unlisted human
or group nouns can be missed; a recognized subject can still have a literal
conditional reading. The object check stops at punctuation or a preceding verb
and can reject a viewpoint reading with an unrelated object in the same clause.

〜にしてみれば has its own rule and identity; it is not consumed by this rule.
Related perspective 〜にしてみたら remains outside this rule's coverage.
