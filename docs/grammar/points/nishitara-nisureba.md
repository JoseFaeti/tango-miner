Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + にしたら / にすれば, retaining the
preceding word as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, reservation and identity. The same forms can mean choosing or
making something, so matches can have literal conditional readings. Other
