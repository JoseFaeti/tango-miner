## Verified coverage

The shared 〜を通して / 〜を通じて rule matches adjacent noun or pronoun
+ を通して and polite を通しまして for means, intermediaries, and duration.
The preceding noun remains lexical evidence; following の is outside the span.
Analyzer-based tests cover these forms, boundaries, literal uses, and controls;
reservation and the shared card identity are also tested. See [the combined
point](otooshite-otsuujite.md) for coverage and limitations. Literal uses may
also match, and other spellings and tokenizations remain unverified.
