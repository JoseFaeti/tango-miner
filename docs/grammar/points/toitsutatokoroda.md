# 〜といったところだ

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

An approximate estimate or assessment: roughly, about, or at most.

## Verified behavior

- Matches といった or と言った followed by ところ or 所 and finite だ,
  です, だった or でした. The preceding estimate remains available as
  lexical evidence; the construction itself is reserved for the grammar card.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, card identity and reservation, and nearby
  non-copular and conditional forms. A literal place described as something
  someone said can have the same surface and is not distinguished.
- Also matches omitted-copula assessments at sentence end, before terminal
  punctuation or a final particle, including the reported
  さすが勇者の血を継ぐものといったところかしら。
  The matched span ends at ところ; かしら remains available to its own rule.
  Integration tests verify the existing card identity, reservation and absence
  of the example-listing card. Locative に / で and comma continuations remain
  outside this rule.
- Other endings and variants remain unverified; literal quoted places can
  still share the supported surface forms.
