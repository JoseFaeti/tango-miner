# 〜ごとに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Each or every.

## Verified behavior

- A noun or attributive verb followed by the separate suffix ごと and the
  particle に produces a card; only ごとに is reserved. Focused tests cover
  Sudachi split-mode C, lexical controls, reservation and card evidence.
- Sudachi can combine the noun and suffix into one token, as in 人ごと and
  日毎. Those forms are not matched; splitting them safely remains unverified.
  Other tokenizations and contexts also remain unverified.
