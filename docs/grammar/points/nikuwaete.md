# 〜に加えて

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In addition.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に加えて, including kana spelling and
polite に加えまして. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal
uses such as adding an ingredient to a mixture are structurally ambiguous and
also match. Other attachments and forms remain unverified.
