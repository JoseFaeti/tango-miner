# 〜に反し

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Contrary to; formal.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に反し in a continuative clause, retaining
the preceding noun as lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Adjacent particles, auxiliaries and verbs
are excluded to avoid claiming longer forms such as に反して and に反します.
Literal uses of 反する are not distinguished; other tokenizations and
attachments remain unverified.
