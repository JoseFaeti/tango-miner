# 〜っけ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Recalling or confirming.

## Verified behavior

- Matches an adjacent Sudachi final-particle token っけ after a preceding token,
  including past and non-past predicates and だっけ. Only っけ is reserved,
  preserving preceding lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover predicate forms, lexical and part-of-speech controls,
  whitespace, reservation, examples and identity registration. Initial っけ and
  merged tokenizations are outside this rule's coverage. Context determines the
  precise force; dictionary expression precedence may suppress matches. Precision
  and recall have not been independently evaluated.
