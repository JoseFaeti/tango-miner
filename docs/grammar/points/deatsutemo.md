# 〜であっても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even if or even though, with the formal copula.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent copular で + あっ + て + も after an expression. The
preceding expression remains lexical evidence, and the shorter 〜であって
card can also be emitted. Sudachi tests cover noun and adjectival examples,
lexical 会って and boundaries; integration tests cover reservation and identity.

As with 〜であって, locative readings can share the same analysis and may
match. Other tokenizations and independent precision and recall remain unverified.
