# 〜に関わる

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Relating to or affecting.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に関わる, にかかわる or に拘わる,
including the past 〜関わった. The preceding
noun remains lexical evidence. The connective form is assigned to the separate
[〜にかかわって](nikakawatsute.md) card when both rules are enabled. Sudachi tests cover dictionary and past
forms and exclusion of the separately assigned て form, boundaries, reservation and card identity. The separate に関わらず
construction is excluded. Involvement and affecting senses can be ambiguous;
the matcher does not distinguish them. Negative, polite and other inflected
forms and other analyzer tokenizations remain unverified and unsupported.
