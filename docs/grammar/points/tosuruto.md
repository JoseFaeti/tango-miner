# 〜とすると

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Assuming or consequently.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent とすると after an expression, including clauses and
adjectives. The preceding expression remains available as lexical evidence.
Tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity.

Literal uses such as 代表とすると can also produce this card. Sentence-initial
とすると and other tokenizations are not covered.
