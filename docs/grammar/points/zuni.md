# 〜ずに

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Without doing.

## Verified coverage

Matches an irrealis verb followed by separately tokenized ず and に, including
godan, ichidan, する and 来る examples. The verb remains vocabulary; the grammar
span is ずに. Tests cover gaps, lexical controls, reservation, card examples,
and overlap with 〜ずにはいられない, for which both cards are recorded.

## Limitations

Other tokenizations and auxiliary chains have not been assessed. This structural
match can also occur within longer expressions, including 〜ずにすむ.
