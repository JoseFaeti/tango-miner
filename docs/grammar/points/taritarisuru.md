# 〜たり〜たりする

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Representative actions.

## Verified behavior

- A verb, adjective or auxiliary continuative followed by Sudachi's たり or
  だり particle and adjacent する produces a card for the final particle and
  する. Plain and polite present and past, positive and negative forms are supported;
  one or several representative predicates can precede it. Attached predicate
  tokens remain available as lexical evidence.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, source examples, lexical controls,
  incomplete forms and identity protection. The form also describes alternating
  states. Omitted or separated する, other inflections, and other
  tokenizations remain unimplemented. This is a morphological match and cannot
  distinguish every contextual reading.
