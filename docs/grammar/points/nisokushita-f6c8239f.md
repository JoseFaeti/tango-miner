## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に即した, including kana spelling when
Sudachi recognizes the verb as 即する or 即す. The preceding noun remains
lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation,
identity, and the distinct spelling に則した. A sentence-final past predicate
can also match because the same token sequence is ambiguous; kana spelling can
likewise be ambiguous with 則する. Other inflections and tokenizations are not
covered.
