## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に則した, including kana spelling when
Sudachi recognizes the verb as 則する or 則す. The preceding noun remains
lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer forms, boundaries, reservation,
identity, and the distinct spelling に即した. The matcher also accepts a
sentence-final past predicate because the same token sequence is ambiguous;
kana spelling can likewise be ambiguous with 即する. Other inflections and
tokenizations are not covered.
