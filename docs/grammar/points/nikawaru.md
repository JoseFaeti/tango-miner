# 〜に代わる

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Replacing; serving in place of someone or something.

## Coverage

- Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に + 代わる / かわる in dictionary form,
  including attributive uses. The complement remains available as vocabulary.
- Sudachi-based tests cover both spellings, boundary and particle controls,
  and distinguish 変わる and the separate に代わって construction. Integration
  tests cover reservation and card identity.
- Kana かわる can be ambiguous with other spellings. Inflected forms are not
  covered by this rule; に代わって has a separate card. Further source evidence
  is needed to assess ambiguity and additional forms.
