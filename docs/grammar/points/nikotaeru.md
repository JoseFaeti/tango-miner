# 〜に堪える

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Withstands or worthy of.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun, pronoun or verb + に堪える, に耐える or にたえる
in plain, past, polite affirmative, て and conditional ば forms. The preceding
word remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization,
boundaries, reservation, identity and the separate negative card. Literal
withstanding and worthiness are not distinguished by context. Sudachi can label
に as a copula, so adverbial readings such as 静かに耐える may also match.
Other forms and tokenizations remain unsupported.
