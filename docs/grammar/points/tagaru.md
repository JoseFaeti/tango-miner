# 〜たがる

Status: [~]

## Meaning and tracked scope

apparent desire of another person.

## Verified behavior

- Sudachi full split-mode C analyses with a verb continuative followed by the
  auxiliary たがる support plain, past, negative, negative past, polite,
  polite negative and て forms. The span excludes the attached verb and
  stops at て before an ensuing いる. The separate 〜ている rule can overlap.
- Tests cover source examples, incomplete suffixes, 〜たい, adjective 〜がる,
  unrelated words and preservation of the attached lexical candidate.
- Merged or differently segmented analyses and other inflections remain
  unverified. Apparent desire is an interpretation, not a semantic guarantee;
  dictionary expression precedence can suppress a grammar occurrence.
