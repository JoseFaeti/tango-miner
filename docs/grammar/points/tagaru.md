# 〜たがる

Status: [ ]

## Meaning and tracked scope

apparent desire of another person.

## Proposed investigation (unverified)

- Check verb + たがる and its past, negative and polite forms, including
  split and merged Sudachi analyses. Verify actual tokenization and meaning.
- Test lexical uses of たがる and unrelated words containing similar sounds;
  preserve useful lexical candidates and attached verbs.
- Investigate overlap with [〜たい](tai.md) and [〜がる](garu.md).
  The [〜たい specification](tai.md), its linked rule and tests are a starting comparison, not
  evidence that the constructions share matching behavior.
