# 〜ずにはいられない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Cannot help doing.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb in the irrealis form followed by ずに(は)いられない,
including 居られない, past, negative polite, continuative and conditional
negative forms with separate Sudachi tokens. The verb remains vocabulary; the
grammar span starts at ず. Tests cover godan, ichidan, する and 来る verbs,
positive and incomplete endings, gaps, reservation and card examples.

## Limitations

The construction can also describe a literal inability to remain without
doing something; token matching cannot distinguish that reading. Other
segmentations and inflections have not been assessed.
