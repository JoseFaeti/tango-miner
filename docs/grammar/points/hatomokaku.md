# 〜はともかく

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Setting aside; regardless of the first matter, consider another.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent はともかく and は兎も角 after an attached expression,
including nominalized clauses and として continuations. The preceding expression
remains lexical evidence; the particle and adverb are reserved for grammar.
The topic-particle card may also match は. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, lexical controls, reservation and identity.

An independent adverb after a topic, as in 彼はともかく行った, is ambiguous
and can also match. Other spellings and tokenizations are not covered. Dictionary
expression precedence can suppress a match when enabled.
