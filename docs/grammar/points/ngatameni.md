## Meaning

Literary expression of purpose: in order to; for the purpose of.

## Tracked scope

Recognizes an adjacent verbal irrealis and `んがために`, including Sudachi's
`サ変可能` noun plus `せん` analysis, preserving the verb as lexical evidence.
The same span is excluded from the negative `〜ぬ / 〜ん` rule. Tests cover
observed Sudachi forms, controls, boundaries, reservation and card creation.
Other tokenizations and variants such as `んがための` are not covered; corpus
precision and recall have not been assessed.
