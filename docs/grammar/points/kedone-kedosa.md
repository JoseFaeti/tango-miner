# 〜けどね / 〜けどさ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Conversational ending that softens or adds an informal, shared-context tone
to a qualification or contrast.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent けどね and けどさ are matched after a terminal verb, adjective,
  or auxiliary, including tested past, negative, polite, and copular forms.
  The two particles are reserved; the preceding predicate remains available.
- Focused Sudachi and integration tests cover both forms, lexical and
  sentence-initial controls, token boundaries, card examples, and overlap with
  the existing 〜けど / 〜けれども card. Both cards can be emitted.
- The rule inherits the existing けど predicate and tokenization requirements;
  it does not distinguish discourse meanings or require sentence-final position.
  Other spellings and segmentations are not covered. Corpus precision and recall
  have not been measured.
