- [Meaning](#meaning)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

At or only after.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent にして after selected temporal nouns and counters, including
歳, 年 and 一瞬. The temporal expression remains available as lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation
and card identity. にしては and にしても are excluded.

Literal にする can share these tokens, as in 時間にしておく; the matcher cannot
distinguish that reading. Other temporal expressions, tokenizations and the
separate にして初めて construction need further investigation.
