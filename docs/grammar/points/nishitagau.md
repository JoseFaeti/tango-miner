# 〜に従う

[Sections](#meaning) · [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Follow, comply with, or obey a person, rule or instruction.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent noun/pronoun + に従う or にしたがう in dictionary form,
including sentence-final and attributive uses and forms followed by べき or と.
Only the particle and verb are reserved; the preceding noun remains lexical
evidence. Sudachi full dictionary regressions verify token boundaries, both
spellings, pronouns, and negative, past, polite and conditional controls.
Integration tests verify reservation, source examples and stable card identity.

This structural rule also includes literal following of a person; it does not
infer the referent's meaning. Inflected forms are not yet covered. Dictionary
verb + に従う change constructions remain outside the verified scope.
Connectives have separate rules for [〜にしたがい](nishitagai.md) and
[〜にしたがって](nishitagatsute.md).
