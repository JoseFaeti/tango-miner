# 〜に当たり

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

On the occasion of; formal.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by
に当たり or にあたり. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused
tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity.
The rule excludes 人 and 壁 and forms followed by a verb or auxiliary, including
polite inflections. These controls can miss occasion readings; other lexical
uses of 当たる can still match. Polite occasion forms such as に当たりまして
and other tokenizations are not supported.
