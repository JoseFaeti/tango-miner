## Verified behavior and limitations

- Matches an adjacent non-past verb followed by までもない, までもなかった,
  までもありません or までもありませんでした. The span starts at まで,
  retaining the verb as lexical evidence.
- Focused Sudachi tests cover these forms, noun, past and negative verb controls,
  spacing, unrelated endings, reservation and card identity. Literal readings
  can share this sequence; meaning cannot always be distinguished by morphology.
  Other inflections and tokenizations, dictionary precedence, and corpus-wide
  precision and recall remain unverified. までもなく is tracked separately.
