# 〜たところ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Just did or result of trying.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent verb, past auxiliary た/だ and ところ/所 produces a card,
  including result clauses and the recent-completion copular form. The marker
  alone is reserved, preserving the verb and past auxiliary as source evidence.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, lexical controls and card integration.
- Literal places and occasions can have the same form (e.g. 昨日行った所);
  the rule cannot distinguish them from the grammar reading. Copular instances
  also produce the existing 〜ところだ card. Negative and polite past forms
  are not covered, nor is semantic confirmation of a resulting discovery.
