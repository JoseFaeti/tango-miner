## Meaning

Even; to begin with, citing an illustrative example.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent `にしてからが` following a preceding expression, retaining that
expression for mining. Tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation
and card identity. Literal にする followed by てからが can share this analysis;
the matcher does not resolve the meaning. Other tokenizations and separated
forms are not covered.
