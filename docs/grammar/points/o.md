# を

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Object and route.

## Verified coverage

Matches a complete Sudachi `を` case particle (`助詞, 格助詞`) adjacent to
a preceding token, including object, route and departure uses. Only the
particle is reserved; neighboring words remain lexical evidence. Dictionary
expression precedence applies, and longer grammar rules can overlap it, as in
`を抜きにして`.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, lexical and boundary controls, reservation,
overlap, examples and identity. The rule does not distinguish senses.
Standalone and spaced particles, punctuation other than a closing bracket
before the particle, and forms tokenized within a larger token are unsupported.
Corpus precision, recall and effects on lexical candidates have not been measured.
