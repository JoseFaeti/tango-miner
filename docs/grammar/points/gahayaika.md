# 〜が早いか

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

As soon as; the moment that one action happens, another follows.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent dictionary-form verbs and verb past forms followed by が早いか or
  がはやいか produce a card. Only the marker is reserved, retaining the verb.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi split mode C, both forms, lexical controls and
  card evidence.
- This surface match does not establish a subsequent action and can mistake
  a contrastive が followed by a question for the construction. Other verb
  forms and tokenizations remain unverified.
