# 〜なり〜なり

[Meaning](#meaning) · [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Alternative examples or choices, such as doing either of two things.

## Verified coverage

Matches paired `なり` markers following nouns, pronouns or dictionary-form
verbs, including Sudachi full's particle and nominal-suffix analyses. Each
successive pair yields its first marker, leaving the listed words available as
lexical candidates. Sentence boundaries and spaces between a marker and its
preceding word block a match; `なりの` is excluded as a first marker.
Dictionary-expression precedence still applies.

The matcher does not determine whether the context actually presents choices;
other repeated markers can be ambiguous. Complex phrases, other verb forms,
alternative tokenizations, and corpus precision and recall remain unvalidated.
