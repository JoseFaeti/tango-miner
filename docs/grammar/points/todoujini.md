# 〜と同時に

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

At the same time as; also connects simultaneous properties.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent と同時に after nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives and
auxiliaries. The preceding word remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover
Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and card identity. The rule does
not distinguish simultaneous events from simultaneous properties or other
interpretations. Alternative spellings and tokenizations are not covered.
