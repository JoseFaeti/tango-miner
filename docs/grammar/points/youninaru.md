# 〜ようになる

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Change in ability or habit.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb in plain nonpast or verb + ない followed by ようになる,
ようになった, ようになります or ようになりました. Sudachi full-dictionary
tests cover changes in ability and habit, including a negative preceding verb.
The reserved span starts at よう, leaving the preceding verb available as a
lexical candidate. The more specific point owns its overlap with 〜ように.
Noun comparisons, adjective attachments and separated tokens are controls.

## Remaining limitations

The なる side currently covers only positive finite forms; negative, connective
and other inflections such as ようになっている remain unimplemented. Semantic
distinctions between change and other verb-attached readings are not resolved.
