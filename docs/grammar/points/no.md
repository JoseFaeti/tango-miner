# の

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Possession and nominalization.

## Verified coverage

Matches complete source-aligned Sudachi の case particles and nominalizers
attached to a preceding token. This includes noun relationships, possession,
and nominalized predicates. Only の is reserved; neighboring words remain
available. Dictionary-expression precedence applies; overlapping grammar rules
can also record the same の, as with causal ので.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, lexical controls, reservation and card
identity. The rule does not distinguish possession from other noun relations
or nominalization from explanatory constructions. Spaced and standalone
particles, sentence-final particles, and forms inside larger tokens are not
matched. Corpus precision, recall and lexical effects have not been measured.
