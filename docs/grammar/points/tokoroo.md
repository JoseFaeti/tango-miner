# 〜ところを

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

At a particular moment or in a particular situation, including despite it.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent attributive predicate followed by ところ/所 and を matches
  when followed immediately by a supported intervention verb or gratitude.
  The marker and particle are reserved; the preceding predicate remains available.
  Tests cover assistance, interruption, gratitude, nominal readings and card integration.
- The supported verbs are 助ける, 捕まえる, 呼び止める, 起こす and 邪魔する.
  Other expressions of intervention or concession are missed. Even supported
  verbs can have nominal readings, so this lexical approximation cannot reliably
  resolve every ambiguity.
