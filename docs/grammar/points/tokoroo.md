# 〜ところを

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

At a particular moment or in a particular situation, including despite it.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent attributive verb or adjective, or an attributive auxiliary attached
  to a predicate, followed by ところ/所 and を produces a card. The marker
  and particle are reserved; the preceding predicate remains available.
  Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, card integration and lexical controls.
- Literal places can have the same form, such as 昨日行った所を見た, and
  are also matched. The rule does not determine whether the context expresses
  a moment, concession, or a literal place. Other predicate forms and semantic
  disambiguation remain unverified.
