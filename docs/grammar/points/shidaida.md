## Verified behavior

- An adjacent noun or pronoun followed by separately tokenized 次第 or しだい
  and だ, です, だった or でした produces a card. The marker and copula are
  reserved; the preceding word remains available. Focused tests cover Sudachi
  mode C, both spellings, card evidence and lexical and succession controls.
- Combined tokens such as 結果次第 are not split. Other copula forms,
  tokenizations and semantic ambiguities remain unverified; a noun adjacent to
  次第 can also have a lexical reading in context.
