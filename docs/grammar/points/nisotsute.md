# 〜に沿って

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Along or in accordance with.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に沿って, including kana spelling. The
preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms,
boundaries, reservation and identity. Both literal paths and figurative
conformity match. Attributive に沿った and other inflections or tokenizations
are not covered.
