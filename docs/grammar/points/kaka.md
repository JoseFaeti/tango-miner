# 〜か〜か

[Contents](#meaning) | [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Whether one alternative or another.

## Verified behavior

- Matches two separately tokenized か particles after alternatives, with an
  optional comma and a single second predicate followed by auxiliaries. Tests
  cover nouns, adjectives, verbs, negative forms and かどうか as a control.
- The card highlights and reserves only the first か, preserving both
  alternatives as lexical evidence. Multiword second alternatives and other
  tokenizations are not covered. Surface patterns can be ambiguous in context;
  punctuation other than a comma and whitespace between tokens are excluded.
