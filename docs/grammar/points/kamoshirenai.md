# 〜かもしれない

Status: [~]

## Meaning and tracked scope

Possibility: might or may.

## Verified behavior

- Adjacent verb, adjective, na-adjective, noun or auxiliary evidence followed
  by split-mode C か + も + しれる and plain or polite negative forms produces
  a card. Plain and polite past forms are included. Only the construction is
  reserved; the attached word remains lexical. Dictionary expression precedence
  can suppress a match.
- Focused tests cover analyzer forms, lexical controls, reservation and card
  examples. Attachment is morphological, so unusual ambiguous contexts may be
  matched. Other tokenizations and colloquial variants remain unverified;
  かも and かもしれん are tracked separately.
