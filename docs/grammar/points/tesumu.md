# 〜てすむ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Suffices to.

## Verified coverage

Matches a verb's て / で form followed by 済む, including past, polite,
conditional, some negative and connective forms. The preceding verb remains lexical
evidence. Tests cover Sudachi tokenization, unrelated 住む and noun + で,
and card identity. The separate 〜なくて済む construction is excluded.

Context can still be needed to distinguish a sequence of actions from the
sufficiency reading. Terminal すまない / すまなかった and polite negative
すみません forms, including 済 spellings, are excluded because they can be
apologies; this also misses genuine negative sufficiency readings in those forms.
Unusual inflections and tokenizations remain unverified.
