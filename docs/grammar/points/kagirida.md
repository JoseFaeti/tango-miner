# 〜限りだ

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Extremely.

## Verified behavior

- An adjacent attributive i-adjective or na-adjective followed by 限りだ,
  限りです, or the kana spelling produces a card. The adjective remains
  available as lexical evidence; the expression is reserved. Focused tests
  cover Sudachi, spacing, lexical controls, reservation and identity.
- The matcher does not distinguish emotional degree from literal limits in
  adjective contexts. Past, negative and other inflections, and other possible
  constructions remain unverified.
