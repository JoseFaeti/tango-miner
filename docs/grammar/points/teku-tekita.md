# 〜てく / 〜てきた

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Direction or change; distinguish existing 〜てくる.

## Supported forms and remaining work

The [〜てくる](te-kuru.md) rule supports selected split `〜てきた` forms,
including the tested `雨が降ってきた`. The ambiguous contracted `〜てく`
and other tokenizations remain unsupported. The earlier backlog marked this
family `[ ]`; its past-form coverage warrants `[~]` without claiming the
whole family is complete.
