# 〜はおろか

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Let alone; not to mention, with a further item presented as even less likely.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent はおろか and は愚か after an attached expression, including
nominalized clauses. The preceding expression remains lexical evidence; the
matched tokens are reserved for grammar. The topic-particle card can also
match は. Focused tests cover Sudachi forms, spelling, boundaries, adjective
controls, reservation and identity.

An immediately following copula is excluded to protect ordinary 愚か readings.
The rule does not establish that a second item exists or disambiguate every
adjective context. Other tokenizations and punctuation before おろか are not
covered. Dictionary expression precedence can suppress a match when enabled.
