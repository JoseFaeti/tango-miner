# 〜ように言う / 頼む

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Indirect instruction or request.

## Verified coverage

An adjacent nonpast verb, or verb + ない, followed by ように言う or
ように頼む is recognized in selected plain, past, polite, negative and
connective forms. Sudachi full-dictionary tests cover 言った, 言いました,
言わない, 頼んだ, 頼みました, 頼みませんでした and 頼んで, with
noun, adjective, unrelated verb, incomplete and separated-token controls.
The span begins at よう, leaving the instructed verb available as vocabulary.
This rule owns its overlap with the shorter 〜ように rule; dictionary
expression precedence still applies.

## Remaining limitations

Other inflections and tokenizations, including passive or intervening words,
are not covered. Morphology cannot always distinguish an instruction from
other uses of 言う or 頼む. Precision and recall have not been independently
evaluated.
