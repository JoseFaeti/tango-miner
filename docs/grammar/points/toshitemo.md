# 〜としても

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Even supposing.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent `としても` following an expression, including clauses,
adjectives and nouns. The preceding expression remains available as lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and
card identity.

Capacity readings such as 代表としても and attempts such as 帰ろうとしても
share these tokens, so they can also produce this card alongside other rules.
Other tokenizations are not covered. The separate paired construction has its
own rule and may match alongside this one.
