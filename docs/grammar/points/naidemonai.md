# 〜ないでもない

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not impossible; a qualified affirmation, sometimes expressing reluctance.

## Coverage

Recognizes adjacent `ないでもない`, `ないでもなかった`,
`ないでもありません` and `ないでもありませんでした` after negative verb,
adjective, copular and existence predicates. The matched span begins at the
first ない, retaining the preceding predicate as lexical evidence. Sudachi
analyzes で as either a connective particle or a form of だ in these examples.
The ordinary verbal negative and ないで rules can also report overlapping
matches.

Focused Sudachi tests cover these forms, nearby constructions, token boundaries,
reservation and registry identity. A literal reading can be indistinguishable
from a qualified affirmation. Other inflections and contractions remain
unverified and are not matched.
