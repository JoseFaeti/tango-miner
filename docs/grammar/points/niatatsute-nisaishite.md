# 〜にあたって / 〜に際して

Status: [~]

## Meaning

On the occasion of.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent nouns, pronouns and dictionary-form verbs followed by
にあたって, に当たって or に際して. The preceding word remains lexical
evidence. Focused tests cover Sudachi tokenization, boundaries, reservation and
card identity, including に際しての. Literal uses such as 壁に当たって and
仕事にあたっている have the same token pattern and are also matched; the
rule does not distinguish their meaning. Other inflections and tokenizations
are not supported.
