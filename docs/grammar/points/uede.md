# 〜上で

Status: [~]

## Meaning

After or in doing.

## Verified behavior

- A past or attributive verb, or a noun plus の, followed by separately
  tokenized 上で or うえで produces a card. Only 上で is reserved; the
  preceding action or noun remains available as lexical evidence. Focused
  tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, card examples and reservation.
- Noun plus の also matches literal locations such as 机の上で; context is
  needed to distinguish these from the grammatical sense. Other tokenizations,
  including fused expressions, and other verb forms remain unverified.
