# 〜上で

Status: [~]

## Meaning

After or in doing.

## Verified behavior

- A past or attributive verb, or 仕事 or 生活 plus の, followed by separately
  tokenized 上で or うえで produces a card. Only 上で is reserved; the
  preceding action or noun remains available as lexical evidence. Focused
  tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, card examples and reservation.
- Spatial examples including 机の上で, 陸の上で and 海の上で do not match.
  Noun readings are context-dependent: the limited noun list can miss other
  grammatical uses and cannot resolve every ambiguous use of its entries.
  Other tokenizations, including fused expressions, and other verb forms remain
  unverified.
