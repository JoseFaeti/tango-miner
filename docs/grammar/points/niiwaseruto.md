## Meaning

According to someone.

## Coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に言わせると / にいわせると with
Sudachi's split causative tokens. The source noun remains available as
vocabulary. Tests cover both spellings, boundaries, nearby forms, reservation
and card identity.

Literal causative and quoted readings can have the same form; ambiguous matches
remain review candidates. Other spellings and tokenizations are unverified.
