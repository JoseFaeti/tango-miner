# 〜と思ったら

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified behavior](#verified-behavior)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

When one thought; contrary to one's expectation, or on realizing something.

## Verified behavior

- Contiguous と思ったら and とおもったら following a token produce a card;
  the preceding thought remains available as lexical evidence. Past verb +
  かと思ったら is left to the separate rapid-succession rule.
- Focused tests cover Sudachi split mode C, spelling, punctuation, lexical
  controls and card reservation. The rule cannot distinguish literal conditional
  thoughts from unexpected discoveries; other tokenizations remain unverified.
