# 〜たるもの

Status: [~]

## Meaning

One who is in a given role; often used to express the duties expected of that role.

## Verified coverage

Matches noun + たる者 or たるもの with Sudachi full's auxiliary たり,
reserving たる者 or たるもの while retaining the role noun. Tests cover both
spellings, lexical 足る and unrelated attributive たる, and card integration.
The broader 〜たる rule also produces a card for the same occurrence.

The noun constraint does not distinguish every literal or non-obligation use,
and alternative analyzer segmentations and spellings are not covered.
