# 〜に至るまで

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Up to and including.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by に至るまで, including kana
spelling. The endpoint remains lexical evidence. Focused tests cover analyzer
forms, boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal uses such as reaching a
station also match; the rule does not distinguish these from inclusive range
uses. Other attachments and tokenizations remain unverified.
