# が

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Subject and focus.

## Verified coverage

Matches a complete Sudachi `が` case particle (`助詞, 格助詞`) adjacent to
a preceding token, including subjects and objects of predicates such as 好き.
Only the particle is reserved; neighboring words remain lexical evidence.
Dictionary-expression precedence applies, and longer grammar rules can overlap
the particle, as in `ことができる`.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, connective and lexical controls,
reservation, overlap, examples, and identity. The rule does not distinguish
subject from object or focus. Standalone and spaced particles, punctuation
other than a closing bracket before the particle, and forms tokenized within a
larger token are unsupported. Corpus precision, recall, and effects on lexical
candidates have not been measured.
