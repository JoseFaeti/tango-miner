# 〜に照らして

Status: [~]

## Meaning

In light of; judging against a standard or experience.

## Verified coverage

Matches adjacent noun or pronoun + に照らして, including kana spelling when
recognized as 照らす. The preceding noun remains lexical evidence. Focused tests
cover Sudachi forms, boundaries, reservation and identity. Literal illumination
uses of に照らして are structurally ambiguous and may also match. Attributive
に照らした and other inflections or tokenizations are not covered.
