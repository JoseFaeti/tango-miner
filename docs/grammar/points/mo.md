# も

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Inclusion.

## Verified coverage

Matches a complete Sudachi `も` focus particle (`助詞, 係助詞`) adjacent
to a preceding token, including additive, negative-polarity, quantity and
particle combinations such as `にも`. Only `も` is reserved; attached words
remain available as lexical evidence. Dictionary-expression precedence applies,
and longer grammar rules may overlap, including concessive `ても`.

Focused tests cover analyzer forms, lexical controls, reservation, examples,
overlap and registry identity. The rule does not distinguish inclusion from
other uses, so it can produce an inclusion card for concessive or emphatic uses.
Standalone and spaced particles, punctuation other than a closing bracket before
the particle, and forms tokenized as part of a larger token are unsupported.
Corpus precision, recall and effects on lexical candidates have not been measured.
