# 〜ないものでもない

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Coverage](#coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Not impossible; a qualified affirmation, sometimes expressing reluctance.

## Coverage

Recognizes adjacent `ないものでもない`, `ないものでもなかった`, and
`ないものでもありません` after negative predicates. The span starts at the
first ない, retaining the preceding predicate as lexical evidence. Focused
Sudachi tests cover these forms, boundaries, reservation and registry identity.
Literal readings may be indistinguishable. Other spellings, contractions and
inflections remain unverified.
