# 〜を問わず / 〜を問わない

- [Meaning](#meaning)
- [Verified coverage](#verified-coverage)

Status: [~]

## Meaning

Regardless of.

## Verified coverage

Matches an adjacent noun or pronoun followed by を問わず or を問わない,
including kana とわず and とわない. The preceding word remains lexical evidence.
The existing [〜を問わず](otowazu.md) card also collects the ず form.
Focused tests cover Sudachi split-mode C, boundaries, reservation and identity.
を問わずに is excluded; other literal negative uses can be indistinguishable
from the grammar. Inflections of 問わない and other tokenizations remain unverified.
