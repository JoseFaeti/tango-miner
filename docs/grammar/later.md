# Later grammar candidates

These families are an unordered backlog; their frequency and usefulness have
not been measured. Some overlap one another or scheduled points and need
investigation before consolidation. A family entry does not prove every
related form is unsupported, or that a separate card would be useful.

[OpenJLPT](https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT) is a cross-check, not the source
of these project-authored descriptions. Its reported 526 points and approximate
levels are broader context. This backlog also includes expressions and
colloquialisms beyond JLPT coverage. Prioritize source occurrences and assess
ambiguity and recall before implementing a rule.
